## Article summary
President Donald Trump is consulting with his national security team regarding Iran amid ongoing protests and a reported rising death toll. Trump has expressed belief that the Iranian government is "badly misbehaving" and has called on Tehran to "show humanity." He announced the cancellation of talks with Iranian officials and stated that "help is on its way" for the protesters, though he did not specify the nature of this assistance. Human rights monitors report that over 2,000 Iranians have been killed and 16,700 arrested since the protests began on December 28. Trump has repeatedly threatened military action against Iran if deadly force is used against demonstrators, despite warnings from Iran that the U.S. military and Israel would be legitimate targets. The protests, spurred by the collapse of the Iranian currency, represent the largest demonstrations in Iran in years and a significant challenge to the rule of Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei. The situation in Iran is unfolding as Trump is also managing other foreign policy issues, including the aftermath of a raid in Venezuela, efforts to broker peace between Israel and Hamas, and the war in Eastern Europe.
