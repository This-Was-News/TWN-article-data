## Article summary
The Federal Trade Commission (FTC) has filed a lawsuit accusing the AI-powered search engine Pearl, operated by JustAnswer, of "rampant consumer deception." The FTC alleges that Pearl tricks users into recurring charges they did not intend to sign up for. Users clicking on ads related to their searches are directed to landing pages for services like JustAnswer.com and AskALawyer.com. An assistant chatbot named Pearl then prompts users to join a question-and-answer service for a small fee, but immediately charges a much higher monthly subscription fee, which can be up to $79, without clear disclosure. This practice, described as using "dark patterns," allegedly ensnared hundreds of thousands of consumers, leading to numerous complaints about "scammy" behavior. The lawsuit claims the company's CEO was aware of these practices and did not make changes. JustAnswer disputes the claims, stating they clearly publish pricing and make cancellations easy. The FTC is seeking an injunction against the company for violating consumer protection laws. This case is highlighted as an example of how "dark patterns" continue to be a profitable method for deceiving consumers online.
