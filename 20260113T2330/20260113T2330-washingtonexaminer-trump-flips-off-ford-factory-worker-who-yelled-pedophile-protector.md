## Article summary
President Donald Trump made an obscene gesture towards a Ford factory worker who yelled "pedophile protector" at him during a visit to a Michigan plant. A video captured Trump responding with a mouthed "f*** you" and a middle finger before walking away. The White House confirmed the gesture, stating the President gave an "appropriate and unambiguous response" to a "lunatic wildly screaming expletives." The incident occurred during a trip focused on domestic manufacturing, a key presidential priority. While Democrats criticized the response as unprofessional, supporters found it amusing and relatable. Influencer Nick Sortor expressed his approval, stating, "THAT'S the President Trump I know and love." Despite the exchange, Trump also engaged in positive interactions, taking selfies and exchanging pleasantries with other factory workers.
