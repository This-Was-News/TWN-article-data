## Article summary
The House of Representatives has passed the SHOWER Act, a bill aimed at loosening federal limits on showerhead water flow. This legislation, sponsored by Rep. Russell Fry, seeks to reverse energy efficiency standards reimplemented by the Biden administration, which were originally set by the Obama administration. Former President Donald Trump had previously criticized these standards, arguing that the low water flow made it difficult to wash his hair properly. The bill clarifies that each nozzle in a multi-head shower system can operate at up to 2.5 gallons of water per minute, and aims to prevent future reinterpretations of these standards. Supporters of the bill argue it defends consumer choice and pushes back against regulatory overreach. This action is part of broader Republican efforts to roll back energy efficiency standards on various appliances. Earlier, the House also passed the Affordable HOMES Act, which would remove the Department of Energy's authority over manufactured housing energy efficiency standards. Republicans contend that these energy efficiency regulations increase costs and limit consumer options.
