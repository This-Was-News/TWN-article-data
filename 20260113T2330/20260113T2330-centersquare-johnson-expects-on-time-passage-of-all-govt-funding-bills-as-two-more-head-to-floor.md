## Article summary
House Speaker Mike Johnson expressed confidence that Congress will pass all 12 government funding bills by the January 30 deadline, avoiding a Continuing Resolution and a government shutdown. This optimism follows the House Budget Committee advancing two more appropriations bills, totaling $76 billion, to the floor for a vote. These bills cover funding for departments like State, Treasury, IRS, and national security agencies, among others. While not perfect, the package received bipartisan support, with Democrats highlighting increased election security grants and international humanitarian aid, and Republicans emphasizing taxpayer savings through spending cuts. This development marks progress in the effort to pass appropriations bills, with three already signed into law and three more passed by the House last week. The Senate Majority Leader plans to keep senators in session to pass their version of a three-bill minibus, which includes appropriations for Commerce, Justice, Energy, Interior, and the EPA. Johnson aims for all bills to be completed before the deadline, ensuring they are acceptable to both parties and do not result in "worst fears and nightmares" coming true.
