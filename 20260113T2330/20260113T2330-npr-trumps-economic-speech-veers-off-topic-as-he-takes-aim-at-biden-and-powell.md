## Article summary
President Trump delivered a speech at the Detroit Economic Club, initially intended to focus on the economy, but it quickly veered into various other topics. He claimed the American economy was experiencing record-breaking success, despite recent polling indicating historic lows in his approval rating on the economy. Trump criticized former President Joe Biden for inflation and Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell, even threatening Powell's position. He also touched upon plans to combat fraud, restrict federal payments to sanctuary cities, and cap credit card fees. The speech also included remarks about Minnesota's Somali population and Minneapolis protesters. Trump highlighted tariffs as a key factor in his perceived economic success, particularly mentioning a 25% tariff on foreign automobiles benefiting Michigan. He visited a Ford facility before his address. The speech occurred amidst growing voter concerns about high costs and affordability, an issue that impacted recent elections and is expected to be significant in upcoming midterms. The White House has recently promoted lower gas prices and mortgage rates as economic achievements, and Trump plans to discuss affordable housing in Davos.
