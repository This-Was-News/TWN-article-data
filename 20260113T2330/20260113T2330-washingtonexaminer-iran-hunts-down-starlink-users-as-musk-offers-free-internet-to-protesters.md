## Article summary
The Iranian government is actively hunting down users of Elon Musk's Starlink internet service amidst ongoing deadly protests. Starlink terminals, crucial for communication in areas with limited internet access, have become a vital tool for protesters in Iran, enabling them to share news and videos of the crackdown. However, Iran has reportedly employed electronic warfare, possibly with Russian-supplied jammers, to disrupt Starlink connections, especially in protest hotspots like Tehran. Reports indicate that up to 80% of Starlink terminals may be down, and authorities are conducting door-to-door searches to confiscate these devices, which are illegal to possess in Iran. The government has also shut off regular internet access for extended periods, aiming to suppress dissent without international scrutiny. This lack of communication makes it difficult to ascertain the true death toll of the protests, which began in response to economic collapse and have escalated into the deadliest wave since the 1979 revolution. President Trump has pledged to discuss increasing Starlink access for protesters with Elon Musk. The situation highlights the critical role of satellite internet in facilitating communication and awareness during times of political unrest and government suppression.
