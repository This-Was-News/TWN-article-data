## Article summary
Virginia Governor Glenn Youngkin has publicly endorsed Vice President JD Vance for the 2028 presidential election, aligning himself with other prominent Republicans who have voiced support for Vance's potential bid. Youngkin stated his agreement with President Trump and Senator Marco Rubio, believing Vance would be a strong nominee. This endorsement places Youngkin among figures like Turning Point USA CEO Erika Kirk and Representative Anna Paulina Luna, who have also expressed their backing for Vance. Despite this clear support for Vance, Youngkin remained silent regarding his own political future and any aspirations for the presidency. When questioned about his own plans, he emphasized his focus on the upcoming 2026 midterm elections and the importance of delivering for Americans and Virginians. Youngkin is set to leave his position as Virginia governor on January 17th, with Governor-elect Abigail Spanberger preparing to take office following Democratic victories in the 2025 elections. The Republican party appears to be consolidating around Vance, while Youngkin's own political trajectory remains a subject of speculation.
