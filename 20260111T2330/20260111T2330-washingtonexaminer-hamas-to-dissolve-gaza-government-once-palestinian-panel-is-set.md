## Article summary
Hamas has announced its intention to dissolve its government in Gaza once a U.S.-backed Palestinian technocratic leadership committee is installed. The terrorist group has instructed its agencies to transfer all responsibilities to the new body during this transition. Hamas is urging for the swift establishment of this committee as part of a broader peace process, warning that renewed conflict could erupt if progress is stalled. This development comes amid reports that Hamas is rebuilding its military capabilities and recruiting new fighters, despite the requirements of President Trump's 20-point peace plan for Gaza, which includes disarmament. Israel has stated its readiness to forcibly disarm Hamas if the group does not voluntarily surrender its weapons and is contemplating a ground operation. A ceasefire agreement was reached in October, but both sides have since accused each other of violations. The peace plan's second phase remains on hold, awaiting a resolution on Gaza's governance and the fate of the last remaining hostage. The international community, led by President Trump on the Board of Peace, is overseeing the peace process, with potential board member announcements expected soon.
