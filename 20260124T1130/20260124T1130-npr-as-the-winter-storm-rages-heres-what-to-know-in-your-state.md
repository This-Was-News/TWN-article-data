## Article summary
A powerful winter storm is impacting the eastern two-thirds of the U.S., bringing a mix of heavy snow, dangerous ice, and extreme cold from New Mexico to New England. Over 30 states are under weather advisories, with particular concern for "catastrophic ice accumulations" in the Southern Plains, Southeast, and Mid-Atlantic. Travel is severely disrupted, with over 8,000 flights canceled. Many governors have declared states of emergency or preparedness, mobilizing National Guard troops and emergency services. States like Kentucky and Pennsylvania are anticipating some of the heaviest snowfall in years. Cities are opening warming centers and preparing for potential power outages and hazardous road conditions. Residents are advised to stay informed, prepare for prolonged cold, and take precautions for themselves, their homes, and animals. Some areas, like Boston and Pittsburgh, are preparing for intense and long-lasting snowfall, while others, like North Carolina, warn of potential power outages due to ice-laden trees. New York City schools will remain open despite the storm, while Philadelphia schools will be closed on Monday. Texas is bracing for subfreezing temperatures and potential ice or snow, with electricity providers preparing for outages and grocery stores anticipating increased demand.
