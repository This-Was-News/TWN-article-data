## Article summary
The 98th Academy Awards nominations have been announced, featuring a diverse slate of 14 films across six major categories: best picture, best actor and actress, best supporting actor and actress, and best director. This guide provides details on these nominated movies and where to watch them. Among the contenders is "Sinners," a period drama starring Michael B. Jordan, and "One Battle After Another," an action thriller with Leonardo DiCaprio. Guillermo del Toro's "Frankenstein" offers a new take on the classic, while "Marty Supreme" features Timothée Chalamet in a 1950s setting. "Sentimental Value" explores family dynamics, and "Hamnet" delves into the life of William Shakespeare's family. Yorgos Lanthimos's "Bugonia" stars Emma Stone in a conspiracy-driven plot, and "F1" showcases Brad Pitt as a veteran race car driver. "The Secret Agent" is a political thriller set in Brazil, and "Train Dreams" depicts early 20th-century America. "Blue Moon" stars Ethan Hawke as Lorenz Hart, and "If I Had Legs I'd Kick You" features Rose Byrne in a dramatic role. Finally, "Song Sung Blue" pairs Kate Hudson and Hugh Jackman in a musical comedy, and "Weapons" is a horror film about vanishing children. NPR critics have also compiled a list of the best movies and TV shows of 2025.
