## Article summary
The January 2026 U.S. Winter Storm is predicted to bring a significant mix of ice, sleet, and snow, followed by plunging temperatures across a vast portion of the country. Forecasts indicate widespread snowfall, with areas from the Rockies to Maine potentially receiving nearly a foot of snow. The Northeast is bracing for 12 to 18 inches, with some localized areas possibly seeing two feet or more due to a slowly pivoting snow band. A large corridor from Oklahoma into Ohio could experience over 12 inches of snow, which is considered rare for a single storm in these regions. The ice forecast is also concerning, with projections of up to an inch of accumulation, posing hazardous travel conditions. Freezing rain is highlighted as particularly dangerous due to its ability to accumulate and add significant weight. Much of the nation is expected to freeze, with subzero temperatures forecast from the Northern Plains through the Northeast, potentially reaching as far south as the Gulf Coast and breaking long-standing temperature records.
