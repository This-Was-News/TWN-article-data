## Article summary
A significant winter storm is poised to impact nearly half of the United States, bringing a dangerous combination of heavy snow, ice, and extreme cold. Millions are urged to stay home as airlines have canceled thousands of flights, and transit systems may be disrupted. Cities are facing a salt shortage, potentially making roads more hazardous. The storm's path is expected to move east, with varying forecasts of ice and snow across different regions, while extreme cold is predicted to persist into next week. Power providers are warning of potential long outages, and in Canada, bone-chilling temperatures are expected, with frostbite a serious risk. Residents are advised to prepare by stocking up on food, water, and supplies, ensuring heating sources are safe, protecting pipes from freezing, and having backup lighting. Pet owners are also reminded to prepare for their animals' needs. After the storm, it's important to check for damage and clear snow from critical areas like gutters and vents.
