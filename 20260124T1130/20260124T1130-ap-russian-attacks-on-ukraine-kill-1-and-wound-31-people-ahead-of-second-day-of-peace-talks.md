## Article summary
Russian attacks on Ukraine resulted in one death and 31 injuries overnight into Saturday, coinciding with the second day of peace talks in the United Arab Emirates. Kyiv reported one fatality and four wounded from drone attacks, while Kharkiv sustained 27 injuries from similar assaults. These negotiations, involving officials from Ukraine, Russia, and the Trump administration, aim to advance a peace process to end Russia's nearly four-year invasion. The Ukrainian Foreign Minister criticized Russian President Vladimir Putin for ordering the strikes during the talks, stating that his missiles hit both people and the negotiation table. Despite recent diplomatic efforts, significant obstacles, particularly territorial issues, remain unresolved. President Zelenskyy had previously indicated that a peace deal was close, but the Kremlin insists on Kyiv withdrawing troops from annexed eastern territories. The talks in Abu Dhabi are part of broader efforts to promote dialogue and find political solutions to the conflict.
