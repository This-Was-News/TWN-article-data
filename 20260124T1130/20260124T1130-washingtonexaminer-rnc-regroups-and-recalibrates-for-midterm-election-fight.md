## Article summary
The Republican National Committee (RNC) held its winter meeting in Santa Barbara, California, to strategize for the upcoming midterm elections. RNC Chair Joe Gruters expressed optimism about the party's focus and energy, despite facing internal divisions and external controversies. Debates surrounding the Trump administration's foreign policy, including its stance on Greenland and criticism of NATO, have emerged as potential distractions. Domestic issues such as healthcare, affordability, and immigration enforcement also present challenges for the party. Gruters proposed a large-scale GOP midterm convention to highlight the administration's achievements. The RNC unanimously approved a rule change to allow for such a convention. Some RNC members believe the administration should emphasize how foreign policy, like the Greenland acquisition, directly benefits Americans domestically. Others, like Ben Proto, chairman of the Connecticut Republican Party, stressed the importance of an "America First" message focused on affordability and the economy, arguing that voters ultimately prioritize their personal financial well-being. The party aims to message effectively to convince voters that Trump and Republicans have fostered greater prosperity.
