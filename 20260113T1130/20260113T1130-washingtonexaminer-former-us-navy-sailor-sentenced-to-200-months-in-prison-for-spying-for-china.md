## Article summary
A former U.S. Navy sailor, Jinchao Wei, has been sentenced to 200 months in prison for spying for China. Wei, who served on the USS Essex, was found guilty of selling instructional and procedural information, including thousands of pages of technical and operational data about U.S. Navy ships, to Chinese intelligence operatives. He was contacted via social media in February 2022 and received $12,000 over 18 months for his activities. Wei's role as a machinists mate and his security clearance made him a valuable target for espionage. He was convicted on six charges, including espionage and conspiracy to commit espionage, violating the Arms Export Control Act and International Traffic in Arms Regulations. Officials from the Department of Justice, Naval Criminal Investigative Service, and FBI emphasized that Wei betrayed his oath, his fellow service members, and the national security of the United States. The sentencing highlights the commitment of U.S. law enforcement to prosecuting those who compromise sensitive defense information for personal gain.
