## Article summary
President Trump is exploring diplomatic channels with Iran while simultaneously considering military strike options to deter Iranian leaders from further lethal force against protesters. The Pentagon has presented a range of strike options, including targeting Iran's nuclear program and ballistic missile sites, though cyberattacks or strikes on domestic security forces are considered more likely. The White House emphasizes diplomacy as the president's first option, noting that private messages from Iran differ from their public rhetoric. Trump has indicated that Iran has crossed a "red line" by killing protesters and is considering retaliatory measures, including potential tariffs on countries trading with Iran. Meanwhile, Iran has attempted to temper its anti-American rhetoric, stating readiness for fair negotiations and claiming communication channels remain open. Some U.S. officials suspect Iran may be delaying rather than genuinely engaging in diplomacy. Iran is currently facing significant internal pressure from widespread protests over economic dissatisfaction and years of government discontent, met with a severe crackdown by security forces. The situation has led to increased threats from Trump, who has demonstrated a willingness to use military options when deemed necessary.
