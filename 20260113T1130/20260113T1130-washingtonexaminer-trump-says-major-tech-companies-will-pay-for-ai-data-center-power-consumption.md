## Article summary
President Donald Trump announced that major technology companies will commit to covering the costs associated with the power consumption of their data centers, preventing utility bills from rising for American consumers. Trump emphasized that while data centers are crucial for the artificial intelligence boom, the companies building them must "pay their own way." This initiative aims to address concerns about the cost of living and comes as demand for AI has surged, straining the nation's energy grid. While some residents in areas with high data center concentration have blamed them for increased power costs, some energy companies and a Wall Street Journal analysis suggest data centers are not solely responsible and may even help stabilize prices. Energy Secretary Chris Wright argued that data centers can lower electricity prices by financing new energy infrastructure and smoothing demand. The administration believes that business-friendly states that have facilitated data center construction have experienced lower electricity price increases compared to states that have restricted new energy capacity.
