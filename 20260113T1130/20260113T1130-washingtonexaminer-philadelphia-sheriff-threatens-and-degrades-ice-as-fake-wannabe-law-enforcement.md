## Article summary
Philadelphia Sheriff Rochelle Bilal has publicly denounced Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers, labeling them as "fake, wannabe law enforcement." Her strong remarks were made in response to the death of Renee Good in Minnesota, despite Philadelphia officials having no involvement in the incident. Bilal asserted that "real" law enforcement professionals do not wear masks and criticized ICE agents for actions such as shooting at moving vehicles or standing in front of them. She pledged that ICE agents would be arrested if they committed crimes in Philadelphia and would not be "whisked away" to hide their identities. Bilal also referred to the president as "the criminal in the White House" and stated that he would not be able to prevent ICE agents from facing jail time if they committed offenses. She concluded by issuing a direct threat to ICE, warning them not to engage in criminal activity within the city.
