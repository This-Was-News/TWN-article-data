## Article summary
Accounts of a brutal crackdown are emerging from Iran, with eyewitnesses reporting that government forces are firing on unarmed protesters, resulting in a high number of casualties. Videos and witness testimonies suggest a "shoot to kill" approach, with injuries ranging from pellet wounds to severe gunshot wounds and skull fractures. A near-total communication blackout has made it difficult to ascertain the exact death toll, but human rights groups estimate hundreds, possibly over a thousand, have been killed. Families are reportedly being forced to confess that "terrorists" killed their loved ones, and hospitals are allegedly being monitored by security forces searching for wounded protesters. Despite the violence, some protesters express a lack of fear, believing the killings will not silence their movement. The Iranian authorities have begun to frame the unrest as being instigated by terrorists and foreign agents, while simultaneously acknowledging significant casualties, though often attributing them to security forces.
