## Article summary
Russia has launched another significant drone and missile attack on Ukraine, targeting its power grid and other infrastructure. This assault, occurring just four days after a similar large-scale attack, resulted in at least six deaths and 11 injuries across Odesa and Kharkiv. In the Kyiv region, hundreds of thousands of households lost power amid freezing temperatures. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy reported that Russia fired nearly 300 drones, 18 ballistic missiles, and seven cruise missiles across eight regions. The United States condemned Russia's actions as an "escalation" amidst ongoing peace negotiation efforts. Russia's strategy has been to deny Ukrainian civilians essential services like heat and water during winter, a tactic Ukrainian officials term "weaponizing winter." In the Kharkiv region, a strike on a mail depot killed four people. In Odesa, six people were injured, and energy infrastructure, a hospital, a kindergarten, and residential buildings were damaged. Ukraine is appealing for faster deliveries of air defense systems from the U.S. and Europe. Meanwhile, Russia reported shooting down 11 Ukrainian drones, with Ukraine claiming its domestically-produced drones hit a drone manufacturing facility in Taganrog.
