## Article summary
The Big Bend U.S. Customs and Border Protection sector in Texas has made history under both the Biden and Trump administrations. Under the Biden administration, the sector unveiled the GRAB 350, a first-of-its-kind advanced vehicle barrier system designed to stop high-speed vehicles at the Sierra Blanca immigration checkpoint. This technology aims to enhance public and agent safety by reducing the likelihood of dangerous pursuits. The Big Bend Sector covers a vast geographical area, presenting significant challenges for Border Patrol agents due to its rugged terrain and historically limited resources. Despite these difficulties, the sector apprehended a record nearly 12,000 illegal border crossers in 2023, with over 9,100 evading capture. In contrast, the Trump administration saw a surge in resources and a historic drop in illegal border crossings, with just over 3,000 apprehensions reported in fiscal year 2025. This decrease was attributed to decisive border security policies, enhanced detection capabilities, and the deployment of surveillance technologies. The sector also experienced a unique event under the Trump administration: the birth of the first horse in the Border Patrol's 100-year history within its horse patrol detachment.
