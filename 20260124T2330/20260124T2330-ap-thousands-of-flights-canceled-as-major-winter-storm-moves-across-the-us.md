## Article summary
A major winter storm is causing widespread disruption across the United States, leading to thousands of flight cancellations and snarling major roadways with dangerous ice. Approximately 140 million people, over 40% of the U.S. population, are under winter storm warnings from New Mexico to New England. The storm is forecast to bring heavy snow, sleet, and freezing rain from Saturday to Monday, with some areas bracing for the biggest ice storm in a decade. Power outages are affecting hundreds of thousands, particularly in Texas and Louisiana, due to downed power lines caused by ice-laden trees. Airports like Dallas-Fort Worth International and Will Rogers International in Oklahoma City have seen extensive cancellations. Officials are urging residents to stay home and prepare for potentially prolonged disruptions. In the Midwest, frigid temperatures are creating dangerous wind chills, with some areas experiencing record cold. Many events, including Mardi Gras parades and school classes, have been canceled or rescheduled. Meteorologists describe the storm as unique due to its vast spatial coverage and the extreme cold expected to follow. Emergency declarations have been issued for numerous states, and federal resources are being pre-positioned to assist with recovery efforts.
