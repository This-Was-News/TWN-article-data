## Article summary
Vice President JD Vance is actively campaigning for the Republican party in the lead-up to the midterms, aiming to promote President Trump's agenda. Vance's efforts focus on key issues such as affordability and immigration, as demonstrated during his recent trips to Ohio and Minnesota. In Ohio, he addressed concerns about the cost of living, emphasizing the administration's efforts to improve take-home pay. In Minnesota, Vance met with immigration officials and local leaders to discuss the enforcement of Trump's deportation policies, while also addressing recent violence involving ICE agents. Vance positioned himself as a loyal supporter of President Trump, stating his commitment to executing the president's agenda and offering honest counsel. He also highlighted the importance of Congress taking action on affordability issues in housing and healthcare. Despite the challenging political climate and the upcoming midterms, Vance remains focused on his current role, deflecting questions about future political aspirations. His campaign strategy involves connecting administration policies to voters' daily lives and reassuring them that improvements are underway, even as challenges remain. Vance's efforts aim to bolster Republican support by showcasing the administration's work on economic issues and immigration, positioning himself as a key figure for the party's future.
