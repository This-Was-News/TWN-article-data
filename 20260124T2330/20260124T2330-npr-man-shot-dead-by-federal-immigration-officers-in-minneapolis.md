## Article summary
A man was fatally shot by federal immigration officers in Minneapolis on Saturday, January 24, 2026. The Department of Homeland Security stated that the officers were conducting a targeted operation against an undocumented and armed individual wanted for violent assault. According to DHS, another individual approached the federal agents with a handgun and resisted attempts to disarm him. The agency claimed the officer feared for his life and fired defensive shots. However, Minneapolis Police Chief Brian O'Hara identified the victim as a 37-year-old white male U.S. citizen with no known criminal history beyond traffic tickets, suggesting he was a lawful gun owner. This incident marks the third shooting involving federal immigration agents in Minneapolis this year. Previous incidents include the fatal shooting of Renee Macklin Good on January 7 and the shooting of Julio Cesar Sosa-Celia on January 14. Governor Tim Walz expressed his dismay and called on President Trump to end the immigration operation.
