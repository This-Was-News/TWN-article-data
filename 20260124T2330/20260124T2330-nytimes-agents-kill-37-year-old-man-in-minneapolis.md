## Article summary
Federal agents in Minneapolis shot and killed Alex Jeffrey Pretti, a 37-year-old intensive-care nurse, on Saturday morning. Videos of the incident appear to contradict federal officials' claims that Mr. Pretti approached agents with a handgun and intent to harm them. Instead, the footage shows Mr. Pretti stepping between a woman and an agent, being pepper-sprayed, and his concealed weapon being found only after he was restrained. Federal officials, including Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem, labeled Mr. Pretti a domestic terrorist, a claim disputed by Minnesota officials and contradicted by bystander videos. Minnesota law enforcement investigators were initially barred from the scene. The shooting has intensified protests against federal immigration enforcement in the city, leading to the deployment of the National Guard. Local leaders, including Mayor Jacob Frey and Governor Tim Walz, have criticized the federal government's actions and rhetoric, with the city planning to seek a federal judge's order to end the immigration crackdown. Mr. Pretti was described by colleagues and a neighbor as a kind, helpful, and non-violent individual with a valid firearms permit, legally allowed to carry a gun openly in Minnesota. The incident is part of a broader conflict between federal agents and Minneapolis residents, with some Democratic lawmakers vowing to block funding for the Department of Homeland Security in response.
