## Article summary
Chaos erupted in Minneapolis following the fatal shooting of Alex Pretti, a 37-year-old man, by Border Patrol agents. Pretti was killed after allegedly attempting to intervene in an immigration-related arrest while armed. Protests and riots ensued, with Minneapolis police issuing warnings for the crowd to disperse. Footage from the scene depicted federal agents deploying tear gas and crowd control devices amidst screaming locals, and multiple arrests were made. The Department of Homeland Security reported that an officer had a finger bitten off by rioters. Protesters reportedly used trash cans to create a defensive wall against police. State and local officials, including Governor Tim Walz and Mayor Jacob Frey, condemned the federal operation and demanded the removal of ICE and other federal agencies from the state. White House officials, however, accused Minnesota Democrats of inciting chaos to distract from a state fraud scandal. Border Patrol chief Greg Bovino stated the individual appeared to intend maximum damage and a massacre of law enforcement. President Donald Trump also commented, suggesting the incident was a cover-up for financial theft from Minnesota. This marks the third shooting by a federal agent in Minneapolis in recent weeks, and the second fatal one.
