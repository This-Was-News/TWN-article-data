## Article summary
A powerful winter storm is impacting the eastern two-thirds of the United States, bringing a mix of heavy snow, dangerous ice, and extreme cold from New Mexico to New England. Over 30 states are under weather watches, warnings, or advisories, with concerns about catastrophic ice accumulations in several regions. Travel is severely affected, with thousands of flights canceled and delayed. Many states, including Alabama, Arkansas, Delaware, Georgia, Indiana, Louisiana, Mississippi, Missouri, New York, Texas, and Virginia, have declared states of emergency or preparedness. Officials are urging residents to stay weather-aware, prepare for potential power outages, and avoid unnecessary travel. Some areas are experiencing record-breaking cold, with wind chills dropping to dangerous levels. Shelters are being opened, and emergency services are on standby to respond to the severe weather conditions. Schools in some cities, like Philadelphia and New York City, have announced closures or modified schedules. Residents are advised to take precautions to protect themselves, their homes, and their animals from the extreme cold and hazardous conditions.
