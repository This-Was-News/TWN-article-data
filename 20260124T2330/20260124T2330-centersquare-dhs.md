## Article summary
A U.S. Department of Homeland Security (DHS) operation in Minneapolis resulted in the fatal shooting of an armed man by a federal agent on Saturday morning. The DHS stated that the individual approached Border Patrol officers with a handgun during an operation targeting an illegal alien wanted for violent assault. Agents attempted to disarm the suspect, but he violently resisted, leading an agent to fire defensive shots. The man, identified as a 37-year-old Minneapolis resident, was pronounced dead at the scene. Following the incident, protests erupted, leading to clashes with law enforcement. DHS indicated the suspect was carrying two magazines and no identification, suggesting he intended to cause significant harm. Border Patrol Chief Greg Bovino confirmed the man was a noncitizen with a criminal history. Minnesota Governor Tim Walz criticized the federal presence, blaming the "horrific shooting by federal agents" and calling for the President to end the operation and withdraw federal forces. Walz also asserted that the state, not the federal government, should lead the investigation. This shooting marks the third by federal officials in Minneapolis since January. Republicans, meanwhile, blamed local and state officials for the rising tensions and called for the restoration of the rule of law.
