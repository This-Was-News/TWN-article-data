## Article summary
A federal immigration officer shot and killed Alex Jeffrey Pretti, a 37-year-old intensive care nurse, in Minneapolis on Saturday. The incident occurred during a Trump administration immigration crackdown and has sparked protests in the city. Pretti's family stated he was upset by the crackdown and had participated in protests following a previous fatal shooting by an ICE officer. Bystander videos show a confrontation where officers disarmed Pretti, but no videos clearly show him with a weapon. The Department of Homeland Security claims officers fired in self-defense after Pretti approached them with a handgun and resisted. The Minnesota National Guard has been activated to assist local police amid growing demonstrations. The NBA postponed a game between the Timberwolves and Warriors due to safety concerns. Congressional Democrats have sharply criticized the Department of Homeland Security and its leadership, with some calling for impeachment and others opposing funding for the agency. Vice President Vance and former Deputy Chief of Staff Stephen Miller have also commented, with Miller labeling Pretti a domestic terrorist and would-be assassin. The shooting is the second fatal incident involving federal officers and protesters in Minneapolis in January, intensifying tensions in the city.
