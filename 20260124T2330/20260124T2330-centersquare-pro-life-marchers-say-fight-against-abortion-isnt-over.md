## Article summary
Despite the overturning of Roe v. Wade, the March for Life continues as abortion rates have increased and accessibility has grown, partly due to expanded telehealth, mailed abortion pills, and lower costs through virtual clinics. Speaker Mike Johnson highlighted efforts to defund abortion providers through legislation, stating that for the first time, tax dollars are not being funneled to "big abortion." David Bereit of 40 Days for Life explained how Planned Parenthood allegedly uses government funds for legal and political advocacy, freeing up other resources for abortions. Vice President JD Vance announced investigations into Planned Parenthood for fraud related to millions received from the Biden administration. Dr. Ingrid Skop noted that loosened FDA regulations on abortion drugs, which can be ordered online and mailed, lead to unsupervised abortions and increased emergency room visits for women. Bereit asserted that the pro-life movement remains committed to protecting women and children, advocating for a future where abortion is not only unavailable but also unwanted and unthinkable. The fight against abortion is ongoing, with continued efforts to limit tax dollar funding and address alleged fraudulent practices by organizations like Planned Parenthood.
