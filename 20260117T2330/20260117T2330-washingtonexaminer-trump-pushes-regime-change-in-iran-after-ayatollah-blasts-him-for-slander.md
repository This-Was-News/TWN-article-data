## Article summary
President Donald Trump has called for new leadership in Iran following inflammatory social media posts by Iranian Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei. Khamenei blamed the United States for deadly protests in Iran and accused Trump of slander and sedition. Trump responded by stating that Khamenei is guilty of destroying his country and using extreme violence to maintain control. The protests in Iran have reportedly cooled, possibly after the cancellation of hundreds of executions. Khamenei admitted that thousands of protesters have been killed and insists the U.S. must be held accountable for the unrest. The Islamic regime in Iran is unpopular due to economic issues, including high inflation. It remains unclear if the U.S. will intervene militarily, and the Iranian regime has indicated it will not yield without a fight.
