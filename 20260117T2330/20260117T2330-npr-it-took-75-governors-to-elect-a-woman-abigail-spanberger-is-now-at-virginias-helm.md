## Article summary
Abigail Spanberger has made history as the first woman to serve as governor of Virginia. A former CIA officer and three-term congresswoman, Spanberger's inauguration marked a departure from tradition, as she chose not to wear the customary morning suit worn by previous male governors. She aimed for her swearing-in to reflect the modern vibrancy and diversity of the state. Spanberger's victory is seen as a significant moment, with Democrats viewing it as an early test of their "affordability" message. She acknowledged the historical significance of her achievement and paid tribute to pioneering Virginia women in politics, such as Barbara Johns and Mary Sue Terry. Terry, the first woman elected to statewide office in Virginia, shared her own past struggles and expressed hope for future women leaders. Spanberger faces the challenge of navigating Virginia through economic difficulties and federal cuts, with a focus on supporting women and families. She aims to redefine the image of a Virginia governor, particularly for young girls, showcasing that leadership knows no gender.
