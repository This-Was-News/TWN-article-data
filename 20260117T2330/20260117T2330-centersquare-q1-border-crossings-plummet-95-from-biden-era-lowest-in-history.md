## Article summary
In the first quarter of fiscal year 2026, U.S. Customs and Border Protection reported the lowest number of illegal border crossings in U.S. history, with a total of 91,603 encounters nationwide. This figure represents a 95% decrease compared to the first quarter average under the Biden administration, which saw record highs. Specifically, Border Patrol agents apprehended only 21,815 illegal border crossers at the southwest border, a historical low for the first quarter of a fiscal year. In December 2025, nationwide illegal crossings totaled 30,698, the lowest ever recorded for that month. Furthermore, Border Patrol agents released zero illegal border crossers into the country through parole programs in December and for the preceding eight months, a shift from the Biden administration's practices. U.S. Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem stated that America's borders are safer than at any time in history due to President Trump's leadership and the efforts of DHS law enforcement. The numbers reflect a complete reversal from the Biden era, which experienced a minimum of 14 million illegal border crossings, including millions of gotaways and individuals released through various parole and visa programs that have since been terminated or revamped by the Trump administration. The administration is also implementing new policies to identify waste, fraud, and abuse in federal immigration programs.
