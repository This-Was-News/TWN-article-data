## Article summary
Abigail Spanberger delivered her inaugural address as Virginia's 75th governor, marking a historic moment as the first female to hold the office. She emphasized themes of bipartisanship, affordability, and a clear resistance to the Trump administration's policies. Spanberger acknowledged the significance of her achievement, thanking those who paved the way for women in politics. She also celebrated the Democratic sweep of Virginia's top executive positions, with Ghazala Hashmi sworn in as the first Muslim lieutenant governor and Jay Jones as Attorney General. Drawing inspiration from Virginia's history, Spanberger quoted Patrick Henry on unity and recognized former Gov. L. Douglas Wilder. While not naming Trump directly, she criticized the White House for policies impacting healthcare, affordability, and community well-being, stating concerns about rising costs and a "betrayal of values." Spanberger pledged to work tirelessly to lower housing and energy costs, reduce red tape, and ensure healthcare access for Virginians. She received strong applause for her commitment to protecting the state's immigrants, assuring them of safety and security. With Democrats holding majorities in both the House of Delegates and the Senate, Spanberger is poised to steer Virginia in a more progressive direction.
