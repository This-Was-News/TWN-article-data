## Article summary
President Donald Trump made an obscene gesture toward a Ford factory worker who heckled him during a visit to a Michigan plant. The worker yelled, 'Pedophile protector!', and Trump responded by mouthing 'f*** you' and raising his middle finger. The White House confirmed the gesture, stating the President gave an 'appropriate and unambiguous response' to a 'lunatic' screaming expletives. The incident occurred during a trip aimed at highlighting domestic manufacturing. Trump's response drew mixed reactions, with Democrats calling it crass and unprofessional, while supporters found it amusing. Influencer Nick Sortor posted on X, 'THAT'S the President Trump I know and love.' Despite the exchange, Trump also engaged in positive interactions, taking selfies with factory workers.
