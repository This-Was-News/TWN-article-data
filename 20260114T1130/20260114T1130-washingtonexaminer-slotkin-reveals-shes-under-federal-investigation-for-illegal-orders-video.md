## Article summary
Senator Elissa Slotkin has revealed she is under a federal investigation initiated by the U.S. attorney for the District of Columbia, Jeanine Pirro. The investigation stems from her participation in a joint video released in November 2025. In this video, Slotkin and five other congressional Democrats, all with backgrounds in intelligence or the military, stated that members of the intelligence community and the military are not obligated to follow illegal orders. The video warned that threats to the Constitution could originate from within the United States. Slotkin emphasized the seriousness of the legal threats involved. She is the second senator to face scrutiny over the video; Senator Mark Kelly has already been accused by Secretary of War Pete Hegseth of violating military justice codes. Hegseth has initiated proceedings to reduce Kelly's retired grade and pay. Kelly has responded by filing a lawsuit against Hegseth. Pirro's office has declined to confirm or deny the existence of an investigation into Slotkin, and the specific alleged crime remains unclear.
