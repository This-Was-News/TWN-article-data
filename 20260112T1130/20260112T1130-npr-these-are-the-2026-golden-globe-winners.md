## Article summary
The 2026 Golden Globe Awards saw significant wins for both film and television. In the film categories, "Hamnet" secured the award for best drama, while "One Battle After Another" took home best musical or comedy. Timothée Chalamet won best actor in a musical or comedy for "Marty Supreme," and Jessie Buckley was awarded best actress in a drama for "Hamnet." Teyana Taylor received the award for best supporting actress for her role in "One Battle After Another." Paul Thomas Anderson was recognized for both best screenplay and best director for "One Battle After Another." For television, "The Pitt" was named best drama series, and "The Studio" won best musical or comedy series. Noah Wyle earned best actor in a drama series for "The Pitt," and Jean Smart won best actress in a musical or comedy series for "Hacks." The Netflix series "Adolescence" was a major winner, taking home best limited or anthology series and awards for actors Stephen Graham, Owen Cooper, and Erin Doherty. The inaugural award for best podcast went to "Good Hang with Amy Poehler."
