## Article summary
At the 83rd Golden Globes, Hollywood actors and actresses used their platform for political activism by wearing anti-ICE pins. Notable figures like Mark Ruffalo, Ariana Grande, Jean Smart, Wanda Sykes, and Natasha Lyonne sported pins protesting Immigration and Customs Enforcement. One pin honored Renee Good, who died after being shot by an ICE agent, while another featured the phrase "Ice Out." Ruffalo, in particular, used red carpet interviews to criticize ICE and President Donald Trump. He expressed his love for the country but stated that the current situation was "not America" and "not normal anymore." Ruffalo also harshly criticized Trump, calling him a "convicted felon, a convicted rapist," and "the worst human being," questioning the reliance on his morality for the most powerful country in the world. The event highlighted a trend of political statements at Hollywood award shows.
