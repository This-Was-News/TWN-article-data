## Article summary
The Department of Homeland Security (DHS) has released new video footage showing Renee Good blocking traffic with her vehicle during an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) operation in Minneapolis. The video, over three minutes long, captures Good's Honda Pilot obstructing a residential street as ICE officers conduct their operation. Federal vehicles are seen maneuvering around her car, and as Good appears to wave them on, officers approach her vehicle. According to DHS, Good was fatally shot after she allegedly weaponized her vehicle and attempted to run over an officer. This new footage emerges amidst claims from state Democratic lawmakers and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey that Good was attempting to leave the scene and not disrupting the operation. Frey stated on Meet the Press that Good was trying to get out of there and not run over an ICE agent. Minnesota Governor Tim Walz had previously asserted that Good was shot unnecessarily and that the incident was preventable. In contrast, the Trump administration and DHS maintain that Good was actively impeding the law enforcement operation by blocking officers and shouting at them, labeling her an "anti-ICE agitator."
