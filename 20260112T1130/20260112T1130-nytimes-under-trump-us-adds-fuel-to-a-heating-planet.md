## Article summary
The Trump administration is exacerbating global warming by withdrawing the U.S. from the main international climate treaty, seizing Venezuelan crude oil, and reviving the domestic coal industry while hindering clean energy. President Trump has consistently dismissed global warming as a hoax. His administration has closed off avenues for global environmental cooperation and promoted the sale of American fossil fuels worldwide, regardless of the consequences. These actions come after one of the hottest years on record, during which scientists attribute supercharged wildfires, deadly floods, and powerful hurricanes to climate change. The planet is projected to exceed the 1.5-degree Celsius warming threshold, beyond which catastrophic events and species extinction risks significantly increase. The Earth has already warmed by 1.3 to 1.4 degrees Celsius. As a result, the United States is the sole nation to abandon its commitment to limiting warming to 1.5 degrees, with scientists warning that these U.S. actions will impede the global effort to combat climate change.
