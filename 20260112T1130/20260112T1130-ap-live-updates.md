## Article summary
Iran has seen tens of thousands of pro-government supporters rally in Tehran, presenting a show of force following nationwide protests against the country's theocracy. These protests, sparked by economic hardship, have reportedly led to over 10,600 detentions and at least 544 deaths, according to activists. The Iranian government has not released comprehensive casualty figures, and communication blackouts have made independent verification difficult. Meanwhile, U.S. President Donald Trump claims Iran has proposed negotiations after his threats of military action. China has expressed support for Iran's stability and opposition to outside intervention, while Germany has condemned the crackdown. Iran's foreign minister asserts the situation is under control and suggests the protests are a pretext for U.S. intervention. Globally, people have rallied in support of the Iranian protests, with demonstrations occurring in cities like Berlin, Istanbul, Paris, and Washington D.C. In Los Angeles, a truck drove through an anti-government demonstration, leading to a confrontation with protesters. Iran has also summoned the British ambassador over protesters removing the Iranian flag at their embassy in London.
