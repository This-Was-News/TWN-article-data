## Article summary
Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell released a video statement Sunday night to deny criminal wrongdoing after the Department of Justice initiated an investigation into him. The inquiry concerns Senate testimony Powell gave regarding renovations at the Fed headquarters. Powell asserted that the investigation is an unprecedented action, stemming from the Fed's independent interest rate decisions rather than political preferences. He warned that the case could lead to monetary policy being dictated by political pressure instead of economic conditions. Powell stated his decisions were made without political influence, focusing solely on the Fed's mandate. He affirmed his commitment to serving the American people with integrity, despite the threats. This situation highlights a tense relationship between Powell and President Trump, who has previously criticized Powell's interest rate decisions and the cost of Fed headquarters renovations. Trump has denied knowledge of the DOJ investigation. Powell's term as Fed Chair concludes in May, and it remains unclear if the investigation could lead to his removal before then.
