## Article summary
The Justice Department has issued a subpoena to the Federal Reserve, seeking information regarding the central bank's headquarters renovation. Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell stated that this subpoena is part of an escalating pressure campaign by the administration, aimed at influencing the Fed's interest rate decisions. Powell believes the threat of criminal indictments related to his testimony on the renovation project is a pretext. He asserted that the real motive is to pressure the Fed into lowering interest rates more aggressively, rather than allowing the central bank to set rates based on its assessment of the public good. President Trump has been vocal about wanting lower interest rates and has previously threatened to fire Powell. The cost of the Federal Reserve's renovation project has increased significantly. Powell acknowledged that scrutiny of the construction project is valid but suggested the Justice Department's probe has ulterior motives, viewing it within the context of broader administration pressure. The Federal Reserve is designed to be independent of political pressure to make decisions, such as adjusting interest rates, that may be unpopular but are necessary for economic stability.
