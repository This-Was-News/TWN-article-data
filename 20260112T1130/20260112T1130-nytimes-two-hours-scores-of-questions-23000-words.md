## Article summary
In a wide-ranging interview, President Trump discussed his views on presidential power, immigration, foreign policy, and domestic issues. He asserted his authority as commander in chief and detailed his strategy for dealing with threats and resources, particularly in the Western Hemisphere. Trump elaborated on his administration's approach to immigration, emphasizing border security and criticizing the policies of the Biden administration. He also touched upon international relations, including his perspectives on China, Russia, and NATO, and the war in Ukraine. The interview covered his health, plans for White House renovations, and his thoughts on the economy, artificial intelligence, and the upcoming elections. Trump expressed his commitment to making America 'great again' and reiterated his belief in his own policies and leadership.
