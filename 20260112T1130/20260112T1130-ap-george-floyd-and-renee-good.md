## Article summary
Five years after the George Floyd video galvanized a movement, a new incident in Minneapolis involving the shooting of Renee Good by an ICE agent has sparked another debate about law enforcement. However, the clarity and impact of the videos surrounding Good's death are diminished compared to Floyd's. The Trump administration and its supporters quickly established a narrative, labeling Good a "domestic terrorist" and justifying the shooting, while others disputed this interpretation. The available video evidence is inconclusive regarding whether Good's vehicle made contact with the ICE agent before he fired. Experts suggest that the proliferation of more video angles, rather than increasing clarity, may add to the confusion. Furthermore, the rise of AI-generated fake images and the public's increasing desensitization to violence online are contributing to a general distrust of visual media. This environment makes it difficult to discern truth, potentially lessening the impact of such events compared to the George Floyd case.
