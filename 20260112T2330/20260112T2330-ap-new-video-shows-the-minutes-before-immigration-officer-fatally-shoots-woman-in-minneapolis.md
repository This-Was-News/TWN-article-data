## Article summary
A new 3 1/2 minute video, released by the U.S. Department of Homeland Security, shows the moments leading up to an immigration officer fatally shooting Renee Good in Minneapolis. The video, filmed by a bystander, captures federal officers and vehicles on a snowy street as Good repeatedly honks her car horn while blocking the road with her SUV. After unblocking the road and allowing other vehicles to pass, officers approach her vehicle. Another video, filmed by the officer who fired, shows him asking Good to exit her car and attempting to open her door. Good reverses briefly, then turns her steering wheel toward the passenger side as the officer on the driver's side again instructs her to get out. Simultaneously, her wife, standing on the passenger side, urges her to drive. Gunshots are then heard, and Good's SUV crashes into a parked car. Bystanders begin screaming as the officer approaches the crashed vehicle. This incident has sparked national debate regarding self-defense versus recklessness. Related stories mention protests outside the federal building, a judge being asked for an emergency hearing after Congress members were blocked from an ICE facility, and celebrities wearing pins protesting ICE at the Golden Globes.
