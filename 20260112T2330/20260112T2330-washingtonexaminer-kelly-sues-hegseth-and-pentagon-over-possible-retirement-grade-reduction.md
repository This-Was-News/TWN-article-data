## Article summary
Senator Mark Kelly has filed a lawsuit against Secretary of War Pete Hegseth and the Department of the Navy. The lawsuit challenges the legality and constitutionality of proceedings to reduce Kelly's retirement grade and an official censure letter. These actions stem from Kelly's participation in a video with other Democrats, where they reminded service members of their obligation not to follow illegal orders. Kelly argues that the Pentagon's actions infringe upon the constitutional protections of legislative independence and amount to retaliation for protected speech. He asserts that this sets a dangerous precedent, discouraging retired military members from speaking out on matters of public policy for fear of reprisal. The video, released in November 2025, also featured other Democratic lawmakers. Hegseth and President Donald Trump have accused the participants of sowing doubt among service members and committing sedition. Kelly alleges that the review initiated by Hegseth had a predetermined outcome due to prior public comments by Hegseth and Trump. As the only participant still under the Uniform Code of Military Justice, Kelly is the sole target of these Pentagon-initiated consequences.
