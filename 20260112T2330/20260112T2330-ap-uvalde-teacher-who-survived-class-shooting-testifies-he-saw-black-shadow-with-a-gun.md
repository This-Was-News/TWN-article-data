## Article summary
A teacher from Uvalde's Robb Elementary School, Arnulfo Reyes, testified in the trial of former Uvalde schools police officer Adrian Gonzales. Reyes recounted seeing a "black shadow with a gun" enter his classroom and begin shooting, resulting in him being shot in the arm and back. He described praying for the attack to end as the gunman then shot his students, none of whom survived. The trial focuses on Gonzales's actions on the day of the shooting, where he faces charges of child abandonment or endangerment for allegedly failing to act. Prosecutors argue Gonzales, despite his training, did not engage or distract the gunman during the initial moments of the attack. Surveillance video shown in court depicted the gunman entering the school and opening fire, with Gonzales appearing to flinch at the sound of the first shots. Over 370 law enforcement officers responded to the scene, but it took more than an hour for a tactical team to neutralize the gunman. The trial is notable as it's a rare instance of a responding officer facing criminal charges for alleged inaction during a mass shooting. Gonzales has pleaded not guilty to the charges, and his trial is ongoing. Former Uvalde schools Police Chief Pete Arredondo is also facing charges, but his trial date has not yet been set.
