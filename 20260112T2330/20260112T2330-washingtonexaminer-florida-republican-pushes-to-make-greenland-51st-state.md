## Article summary
Florida Republican Rep. Andy Fine has introduced legislation, the Greenland Annexation and Statehood Act, aiming to make Greenland the 51st U.S. state. This move aligns with the Trump administration's expressed interest in acquiring Danish territory due to national security concerns. Fine stated that controlling Greenland is crucial for Arctic shipping lanes and U.S. security, emphasizing the need to prevent hostile regimes from gaining influence. The bill would authorize President Trump to annex Greenland and requires a report to Congress on necessary legal changes for statehood. This follows President Trump's prior suggestion of potential military action to acquire the territory. White House Deputy Chief of Staff Stephen Miller affirmed that Greenland's inclusion in the U.S. has been a formal position of the administration. While facing Democratic opposition, Trump believes Denmark cannot adequately secure Greenland, leaving it vulnerable to Chinese or Russian takeover. Greenland is considered a strategic asset due to its Arctic location, oil reserves, and rare Earth minerals. The U.S. operates the Pituffik Space Base in Greenland and has invested significantly in its infrastructure. Danish leaders acknowledge security concerns regarding Russia and China and are planning to increase their defense budget for the Arctic. Acquiring Greenland would likely cost billions, with the U.S. already providing substantial foreign aid to the territory.
