## Article summary
A study by Melbourne Business School Online has identified Boston, Seattle, and Madison, Wisconsin, as the top cities for students, considering both academic performance and quality of life. The study analyzed factors like grades, pupil-to-teacher ratios, and standardized test scores, alongside community amenities such as housing costs, park access, and walkability. Boston secured the top spot, followed by Seattle, with Madison offering a more affordable yet academically strong alternative. Researchers emphasized that academic success is influenced by the surrounding urban ecosystem, not just classroom conditions. Cities like these excel due to smaller class sizes and supportive living environments, leading to higher graduation rates and test scores. The study highlights a strong correlation between high test scores and broader urban learning environments, including a higher percentage of college-educated adults and better access to resources like libraries. Boston boasts a pupil-to-teacher ratio of 14.8, while Seattle has 16.1 and Madison has 15.9. Boston also leads in American College Testing scores with an average of 26.1, compared to Seattle's 24.5 and Madison's 19.4. Despite these top performers, the broader K-12 education system faces challenges, with national average reading and math scores declining, partly due to the COVID-19 pandemic.
