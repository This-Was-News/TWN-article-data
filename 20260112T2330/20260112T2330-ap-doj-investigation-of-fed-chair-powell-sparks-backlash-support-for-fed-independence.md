## Article summary
The Department of Justice's investigation into Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell, including threats of criminal indictment, has sparked significant backlash and renewed support for the Federal Reserve's independence. Powell himself characterized the DOJ's actions as pretexts to undermine the Fed's autonomy in setting interest rates. This investigation, unprecedented for a sitting Fed chair, has drawn strong defenses from former Fed chairs, economic officials, and even Republican senators. While the White House claims President Trump did not direct the investigation, critics argue it demonstrates an attempt to control the independent agency. The probe, related to Powell's testimony about building renovations, occurs as Trump considers a nominee to replace Powell, whose term as chair ends soon but could remain a Fed governor. Some Senate Republicans have expressed doubts about the administration's actions, with one senator vowing to oppose Fed nominees until the investigation is resolved. Experts warn that a politically controlled Fed could lead to higher inflation and weaker economic growth, potentially impacting job availability and the cost of living. Powell's potential to remain on the Fed board could thwart Trump's ability to shape the agency's future direction.
