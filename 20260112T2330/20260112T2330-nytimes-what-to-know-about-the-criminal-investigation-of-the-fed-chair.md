## Article summary
Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell is under a criminal investigation by the Justice Department concerning a $2.5 billion renovation of the Fed's headquarters. This probe, approved by a Trump ally, is seen by Powell as an attempt to coerce the central bank into lowering borrowing costs, threatening its independence. The investigation focuses on whether Powell misled Congress about the renovation's costs and luxury features. The project, which began in 2022 and is set for completion in 2027, has faced significant cost overruns, attributed by the Fed to material costs, unforeseen issues like asbestos, and tariffs. Powell has defended the renovation as necessary for safety and modernization, refuting claims of excessive luxury. The investigation has drawn widespread criticism from former Fed chairs, Treasury secretaries, and several Republican lawmakers who view it as an unprecedented attack on the Fed's independence and the rule of law. Some fear this could be used as justification to remove Powell from his position. While the President denies knowledge of the probe, White House officials have criticized Powell's job performance. This situation raises concerns about the politicization of the Justice Department and the erosion of institutional independence in the U.S.
