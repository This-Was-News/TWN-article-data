## Article summary
President Donald Trump announced a 25% tariff on any nation conducting business with Iran, effective immediately. This declaration follows escalating protests within Iran and Trump's prior threats of potential military intervention. Trump stated on social media that "This Order is final and conclusive." He had previously warned Iran against violently suppressing protesters, vowing U.S. intervention if such actions occurred. A tariff is defined as a tax on imported goods. Trump has cited his use of tariffs as a tool to end wars globally. However, his use of tariffs under a 1977 law is currently facing a legal challenge at the Supreme Court. This challenge, brought by a group of states and small businesses, could potentially lead to the refund of over $133.5 billion in tariff revenue if the court sides with the plaintiffs. Businesses have reported that these tariffs have resulted in increased consumer prices. Trump has consistently made tariffs a central aspect of his domestic and foreign policy during his second term.
