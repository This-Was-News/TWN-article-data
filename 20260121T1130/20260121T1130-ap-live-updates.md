## Article summary
U.S. President Donald Trump is set to address the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland, leading the largest-ever U.S. delegation. His arrival was delayed due to a minor electrical issue on Air Force One, requiring him to switch to a different aircraft. Trump's assertive foreign policy, particularly his threats regarding Greenland and potential tariffs on European imports, has caused significant tension with European allies. European leaders, including the EU chief and NATO Secretary-General, have expressed concerns that these actions challenge European security and prosperity, and distract from the war in Ukraine. Despite these tensions, trade representatives from the U.S. and EU met to avoid a trade spiral. Celebrities like Matt Damon and David Beckham are also in attendance. Egypt and Israel have agreed to join Trump's Board of Peace, which is expected to be a topic of discussion. Swedish Prime Minister Ulf Kristersson stated that Sweden does not regret joining NATO and will not be blackmailed over Greenland. Danish veterans who served alongside U.S. forces feel betrayed by the threats to seize Greenland. World markets have shown signs of stabilization after an initial drop due to Trump's tariff threats. Egyptian President Abdel-Fattah el-Sissi is in Davos, advocating for international investment in Egypt and reaffirming the Palestinian cause as a regional priority. U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent urged European leaders to await Trump's speech before reacting to his policies. Meanwhile, U.S. special envoy Steve Witkoff plans to discuss the Russia-Ukraine war with Vladimir Putin.
