## Article summary
Maryland's Governors Redistricting Advisory Commission has advanced a new congressional map concept that could lead to Democrats winning all eight of the state's congressional districts. The commission, chaired by Senator Angela Alsobrooks, voted 3-2 to send the map to the General Assembly and Governor Wes Moore. Alsobrooks stated the map reflects public input and aims to ensure adequate representation in Congress, especially in light of redistricting efforts in other states. However, Maryland State Senate President Bill Ferguson has opposed the map, calling it "objectively unconstitutional" and arguing it breaks apart communities more than the current map. House Minority Leader Jason Buckel also criticized the map, labeling the process a "political sham" designed to eliminate Republican representation. Governor Moore expressed anticipation for reviewing the commission's recommendations.
