## Article summary
Air Force One, with President Donald Trump aboard, returned to Joint Base Andrews shortly after departing for the World Economic Forum summit in Davos, Switzerland. The White House pool reported that the crew detected a minor electrical issue and decided to return out of caution. White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt humorously noted that a Qatari jet sounded appealing at the moment. Trump and his team are expected to board a different aircraft to continue their journey to Davos, with his arrival time now uncertain. A government motorcade was observed heading to Joint Base Andrews following the plane's turnaround. The President is slated to address the Davos summit on Wednesday, amidst significant tensions between the U.S. and Europe concerning the former's interest in Greenland. Trump had expressed confidence in his representation at Davos via a Truth Social post prior to his departure.
