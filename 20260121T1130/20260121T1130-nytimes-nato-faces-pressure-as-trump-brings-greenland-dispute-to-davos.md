## Article summary
President Trump's proposal to purchase Greenland has caused global market volatility, with sharp sell-offs in U.S. and Asian assets, and a weakening dollar. Gold prices, however, have surged to record highs, reflecting investor demand for safe havens amid geopolitical uncertainty. NATO leaders are holding frank discussions about the implications of Trump's actions on the alliance, with some expressing concern about a potential fragmentation of the trans-Atlantic partnership. In Minneapolis, federal investigators revealed that a man shot by an ICE agent had assaulted the agent before the shooting, differing from initial reports. Meanwhile, federal prosecutors have subpoenaed several Minnesota officials, including Governor Tim Walz, regarding their response to the Trump administration's immigration crackdown. Separately, Lindsey Halligan has resigned as U.S. attorney in eastern Virginia after a judge ruled her appointment unlawful, ending a prolonged standoff. In El Paso, a Cuban immigrant's family claims he died in ICE custody due to guard misconduct, while federal officials state he died by suicide; the family is seeking to block the deportation of witnesses to the incident.
