## Article summary
President Donald Trump is leading the largest-ever U.S. delegation to the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland. The forum, which brings together over 3,000 global leaders, focuses on emerging technologies, poverty reduction, and climate change. Trump's agenda includes discussions on his ambitions for U.S. control over Greenland, a strategically important territory with vast natural resources. He has proposed acquiring Greenland for national security reasons, citing concerns about China and Russia's interests, and has threatened tariffs on European nations opposing his aims. Trump is also expected to announce details about a new international body called the Board of Peace, intended to oversee Gaza's reconstruction, which he may envision as a larger entity potentially functioning as a U.N. replacement. He will chair this board and is scheduled to address the forum and meet with other world leaders. The U.S. delegation includes key figures such as Secretary of State Marco Rubio and Secretary of the Treasury Scott Bessent. European leaders have expressed solidarity with Greenland and increased their military presence in the Arctic. The theme for this year's forum is 'A Spirit of Dialogue,' following Trump's restructuring of U.S. trade agreements and reshaped international relationships.
