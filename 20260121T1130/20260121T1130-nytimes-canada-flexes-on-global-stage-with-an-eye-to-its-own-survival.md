## Article summary
Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney delivered a powerful speech at Davos, receiving a standing ovation from global leaders. He described the current geopolitical climate as a 'rupture' marking the end of an era dominated by U.S. hegemony, without directly naming President Trump. Carney's remarks coincided with President Trump's escalating rhetoric regarding Greenland and threats of tariffs against European nations. The speech highlighted the resurgence of great power rivalry and the fading of the rules-based international order. Carney emphasized the need for middle powers to unite, stating, 'if you are not at the table, you are on the menu.' He alluded to past U.S. actions, including President Trump's claims on Canada as the 51st state and public derision of former Canadian leader Justin Trudeau. The speech underscored the challenges faced by nations in navigating a world where 'the strong do what they can, and the weak suffer what they must.'
