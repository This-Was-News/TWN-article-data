## Article summary
Air Force One, carrying President Trump, returned to Joint Base Andrews due to a minor electrical issue shortly after takeoff. A White House official stated that the plane experienced some non-functioning cabin lights, prompting the return out of an abundance of caution. President Trump was expected to board a different aircraft to continue his journey to Switzerland for the World Economic Forum in Davos, with an anticipated arrival delay of about three hours. It was uncertain if he would make his scheduled speech. Several senior officials, including Secretary of State Marco Rubio and White House Chief of Staff Susie Wiles, were on board. Air Force One refers to any Air Force plane carrying the president, with two modified Boeing 747s in service, both over 35 years old and prone to maintenance issues, which President Trump has often criticized. An aviation expert noted that electrical systems have backups, suggesting the detected issue was relatively critical. The Trump administration had previously renegotiated a contract for two new 747-8 planes, but they are not expected to be ready before the end of his second term. Additionally, a new 747-8 jetliner received from Qatar requires extensive work before it can be secured for use as Air Force One.
