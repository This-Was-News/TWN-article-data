## Article summary
President Donald Trump, in a press briefing marking his first year back in office, repeated numerous false claims regarding the 2020 election, foreign policy, the economy, and energy. He falsely claimed the 2020 election was rigged, despite evidence and court rulings to the contrary. Trump also exaggerated his role in settling international conflicts, with many of these disputes remaining unresolved or having seen renewed fighting. Regarding the economy, he inaccurately stated that inflation was at an unprecedented high, when historical data shows higher inflation rates in the past. His defense of 'clean, beautiful coal' omitted the environmental impacts beyond carbon dioxide emissions. Finally, Trump's explanation for water issues during California wildfires incorrectly linked it to water diversion for fish, while local officials cited system failures during the disaster. The article fact-checks these claims, providing evidence and context to refute them.
