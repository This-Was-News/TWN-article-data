## Article summary
Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell is set to attend a Supreme Court hearing on Wednesday concerning President Trump's authority to fire Fed Governor Lisa Cook. This appearance comes shortly after the Department of Justice launched a criminal investigation into Powell himself, which he claims is a pretext to influence monetary policy. The Supreme Court case centers on Trump's attempt to remove Cook, who is accused of mortgage fraud, testing the president's power over the central bank. Trump's legal team argues that an "ethically compromised" governor should not wield significant economic power. Powell has publicly stated that the DOJ investigation is an attempt to pressure him into lowering interest rates, which he believes jeopardizes the Fed's independence. Trump has consistently urged the Fed to cut interest rates, while the Fed maintains that premature cuts could lead to inflation. Powell's attendance at the hearing may signal his continued resistance to political interference with the Federal Reserve's decisions.
