## Article summary
President Trump has stated that a reason for his pursuit of Greenland is that he did not receive the Nobel Peace Prize. He conveyed this sentiment in a text message to Norway's prime minister over the weekend. In the message, Trump suggested that since he felt overlooked for the prize for his efforts in stopping wars, he now feels less obligated to prioritize peace and can instead focus on what benefits the United States. He also questioned Denmark's sovereignty over Greenland, asserting that there are no written documents to support their claim and that global security requires complete U.S. control of the island. This message has heightened tensions surrounding Greenland, which has been part of the Danish Kingdom for over 300 years. World leaders have widely condemned Trump's insistence on acquiring the Arctic territory. The situation has introduced a new layer of unpredictability regarding Trump's intentions and his campaign to take control of Greenland.
