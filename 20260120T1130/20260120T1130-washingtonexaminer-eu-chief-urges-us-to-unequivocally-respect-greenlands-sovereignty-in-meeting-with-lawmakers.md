## Article summary
European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen met with U.S. lawmakers, urging them to respect Greenland's sovereignty. This meeting occurred as President Trump continues to express his desire to acquire Greenland from Denmark. Von der Leyen emphasized the importance of respecting Greenland's sovereignty for the trans-Atlantic relationship and stated the EU's readiness to cooperate on shared security interests. She also criticized President Trump's announced tariffs on European nations that do not support the U.S. acquisition of Greenland, calling them counterproductive to shared economic interests. U.S. Senators Chris Coons, Lisa Murkowski, and Thom Tillis also condemned the tariffs, with Coons warning of a potential trade war and damage to NATO. The Trump administration claims acquiring Greenland is vital for national security to counter Russian or Chinese influence. Trump views European countries opposing the acquisition as playing a dangerous game and has imposed tariffs set to affect several European nations starting February 1st.
