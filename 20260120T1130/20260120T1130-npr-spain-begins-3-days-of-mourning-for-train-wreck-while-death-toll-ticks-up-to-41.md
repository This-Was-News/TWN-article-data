## Article summary
Spain has begun three days of national mourning following a devastating high-speed train collision that killed at least 41 people. The accident occurred when the tail end of a train traveling from Malaga to Madrid derailed and struck an oncoming train from Madrid to Huelva. Emergency crews continue to search for more bodies amidst the wreckage, with officials warning the death toll may rise. The cause of the crash is under investigation, but officials have noted a broken section of track as a potential factor, though it remains unconfirmed if it was a cause or consequence. Both trains were reportedly traveling below the speed limit, and human error has been tentatively ruled out. The incident has shaken Spain, a nation with extensive high-speed rail infrastructure. The King and Queen are scheduled to visit the accident site and injured survivors. The crash has caused significant disruptions to train services, leading to increased flights and bus services to assist stranded travelers. DNA samples are being collected from families seeking information about unidentified victims.
