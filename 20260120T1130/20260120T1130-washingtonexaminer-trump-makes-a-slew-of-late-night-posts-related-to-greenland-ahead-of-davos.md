## Article summary
President Donald Trump made a series of late-night social media posts concerning Greenland and other foreign affairs ahead of his visit to the World Economic Forum in Davos. These posts included a summary of a phone call with NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte, where Trump stated Greenland is imperative for national and world security and that a meeting of parties would occur in Davos. Trump also shared fictitious images depicting U.S. control over Greenland and discussions with European leaders. He emphasized the United States' global strength as the sole guarantor of world peace. Additionally, Trump criticized the United Kingdom for planning to cede Diego Garcia to Mauritius, viewing it as an act of weakness and a reason why Greenland must be acquired. He also posted what appeared to be a text message from Rutte praising his foreign policy work and expressing a desire to find a way forward on Greenland. Trump was scheduled to depart for Davos with the largest U.S. delegation in history, where Greenland was expected to be a critical topic of discussion with European leaders.
