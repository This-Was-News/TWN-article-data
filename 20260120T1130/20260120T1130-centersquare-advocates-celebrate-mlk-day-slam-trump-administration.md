## Article summary
Community leaders across the nation gathered to commemorate Martin Luther King Jr. Day, celebrating his life and legacy. Many used the occasion to voice criticism of the Trump administration's policies. Events included peace walks, parades, health fairs, and museum activities. In Atlanta, Ebenezer Baptist Church hosted a service where Bernice King, daughter of Martin Luther King Jr., spoke out against U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement measures and the administration's stance on diversity, equity, and inclusion programs. She compared immigration enforcement to tactics used against civil rights protestors and stated that such actions would not deter their efforts to protest for rights. King also addressed President Trump's comments regarding the Civil Rights Act of 1964, asserting that it was intended to heal the nation, not harm white people. In New York City, House Democratic Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries also criticized the president during an MLK Day event. Parades and celebrations honoring King's dream and commitment to civil rights were also held in Los Angeles and Dallas, emphasizing unity and determination.
