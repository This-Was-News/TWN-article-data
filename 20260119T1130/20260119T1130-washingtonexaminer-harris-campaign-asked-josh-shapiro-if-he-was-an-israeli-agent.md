## Article summary
A new memoir by Pennsylvania Governor Josh Shapiro reveals that Kamala Harris's 2024 presidential campaign vetting team questioned him about his potential ties to the Israeli government. During the vetting process, where Shapiro was a finalist for Harris's running mate, he was asked by a senior member of the team if he had ever been an agent of the Israeli government or communicated with an undercover Israeli agent. Shapiro expressed that he found these questions offensive, particularly given his Jewish heritage. The campaign also inquired about his willingness to apologize for statements made regarding pro-Palestinian protests at the University of Pennsylvania. Shapiro defended his stance on free speech, while acknowledging that some campus demonstrations were not peaceful. He questioned whether these specific inquiries about Israel were unique to him as the only Jewish candidate or if other contenders without federal experience faced similar scrutiny. The memoir, titled _Where We Keep the Light_, details these exchanges, though Shapiro described the overall vetting as professional. This revelation comes as Shapiro is running for reelection as governor and amid speculation about a potential presidential run in 2028.
