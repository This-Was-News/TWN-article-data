## Article summary
Iranian state TV was hacked on Sunday, with multiple channels airing a message from exiled crown prince Reza Pahlavi. Pahlavi urged protesters to continue demonstrations despite the regime's violent crackdown. He also called on security forces to side with the protesters. The cyberattack was shared by Pahlavi's communications office as a call to action. This signals that the protests are far from over, even after the regime's actions appeared to quell demonstrations. President Donald Trump has also voiced support for regime change in Iran, calling for new leadership. Trump referred to Ayatollah Ali Khamenei as a "sick man" who should run his country properly and stop killing people. Khamenei has blamed Trump for the deaths of protesters, calling him a criminal. Iran has vowed all-out war if Khamenei is targeted in any U.S. strike or military mission. If large-scale protests resume, they would increase pressure on the regime amid an economic crisis, but likely lead to a higher death toll. An internet blackout has made it difficult to estimate the death toll, but human rights groups estimate it is in the thousands.
