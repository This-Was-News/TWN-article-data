## Article summary
The struggle for control of Greenland has intensified, raising concerns about the Atlantic alliance. NATO members have historically underinvested in Arctic security, despite increasing threats from melting glaciers and aggressive naval powers. President Trump appears unwilling to seek a collaborative solution to this growing problem. Instead, his actions have created a significant rift within the nearly 77-year-old alliance. This has led to strong reactions, with one European leader stating that nations should not be blackmailed by a major power within the group. Even allies have expressed concern, warning of a dangerous downward spiral. The situation highlights a broader issue of underinvestment in Arctic security by NATO members. The article suggests Trump has an opportunity to de-escalate the situation regarding Greenland but seems disinclined to take it. This geopolitical tension could have lasting consequences for international relations and security in the Arctic region.
