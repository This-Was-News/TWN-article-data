## Article summary
Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado met with U.S. President Donald Trump at the White House, presenting him with her Nobel Peace Prize medal as a recognition for his commitment to freedom. This meeting occurred amidst Venezuela's political transition following the U.S. military capture of former President Nicolás Maduro. Machado also met with senators on Capitol Hill, while Venezuela's acting President Delcy Rodríguez addressed the nation, calling for oil industry reforms and diplomacy with the U.S. Meanwhile, Trump threatened to invoke the Insurrection Act to deploy military forces in Minneapolis to quell protests against federal immigration officers, a move criticized by state officials. The U.S. also continued its actions against Venezuela by seizing another sanctioned oil tanker. In other news, Taiwan agreed to invest $250 billion in the U.S. for lower tariffs, and the U.S. imposed new sanctions on Iranian officials. The White House also outlined a healthcare plan and discussed ongoing investigations into the Labor Secretary. Additionally, a federal judge cleared the way for a New York offshore wind project, marking a victory for the industry against the Trump administration's previous pause orders. Market reactions were influenced by Trump's comments on Iran, leading to a drop in oil prices.
