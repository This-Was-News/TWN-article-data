## Article summary
President Trump announced an outline for new health care legislation called "The Great Healthcare Plan." The plan, presented via a social media video and a White House fact sheet, focuses on four pillars: drug price reforms, health insurance reforms, price transparency, and fraud protections. However, it does not offer specific solutions for individuals facing skyrocketing Affordable Care Act (ACA) premiums, a key concern for many seeking insurance on Healthcare.gov. While Trump and Medicare and Medicaid chief Dr. Mehmet Oz presented the plan as a replacement for the ACA, its scope is narrower than the 2010 law. Experts suggest the plan is a compilation of Republican ideas and does not address rising premium payments. Meanwhile, a bipartisan group of senators, including Bernie Moreno and Lisa Murkowski, continues negotiations to extend ACA enhanced subsidies. Moreno expressed optimism about Trump's plan but blamed Democratic leadership for hindering progress. Murkowski remains hopeful for a bipartisan fix before the end of ACA open enrollment, acknowledging the difficult decisions families face regarding coverage costs.
