## Article summary
A recent poll indicates a significant decline in President Donald Trump's approval rating among Arizonans, dropping 16 percentage points between February and December. Favorability towards Trump fell from +3% to -13%, attributed by pollsters to economic concerns and policy uncertainties. This trend is seen as a warning sign for Republicans in a state decided by narrow margins. Democratic Governor Katie Hobbs also experienced a decrease in her approval rating, falling 5 percentage points to +1%. The poll identified inflation, affordable housing, and healthcare as the top concerns for Arizonans, with immigration seeing a decline in importance. A majority of Arizonans, 52%, believe the state is heading in the wrong direction, a sentiment linked to the rising cost of living. The upcoming gubernatorial race in Arizona is expected to be highly competitive, with polls showing Governor Hobbs in a statistical tie with leading Republican candidates, who are endorsed by Trump. Election analysts rate the Arizona gubernatorial race as a toss-up.
