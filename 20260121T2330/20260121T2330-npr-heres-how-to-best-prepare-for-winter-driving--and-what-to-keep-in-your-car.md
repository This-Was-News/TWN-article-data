## Article summary
To best prepare for winter storm driving, experts advise staying home if possible. If travel is necessary, keep an emergency kit in your car, including a first-aid kit, phone charger, flashlight, nonperishable food, water, and extra pet food if applicable. Blankets, extra clothing, hats, scarves, and gloves are also crucial for warmth and preventing hypothermia. Always ensure your gas tank is full before setting out, as this can help you stay warm if stranded. Leaving your vehicle is generally not recommended, as it provides shelter and heat. Keep exhaust pipes clear of snow and ice to prevent carbon monoxide poisoning. Pay close attention to weather warnings and forecasts, and be prepared to turn back if conditions become too dangerous. Proactive monitoring of weather and heeding official warnings are essential for avoiding perilous situations on the road during winter storms.
