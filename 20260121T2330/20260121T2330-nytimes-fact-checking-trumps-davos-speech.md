## Article summary
President Trump delivered a speech at the World Economic Forum in Switzerland, during which he criticized European allies and reiterated his interest in acquiring Greenland. The speech also contained distortions of Greenland's history, attacks on the North Atlantic Treaty Organization, and familiar claims about the economy and his own performance. A specific point of contention was Trump's statement about the United States giving Greenland back to Denmark after the war, which is misleading. He was likely referencing a World War II defense pact between the U.S. and Denmark. This agreement, forged in 1941 after Denmark's invasion by Nazis, granted the U.S. rights to military bases in Greenland in return for protection of the island. However, the pact did not transfer sovereignty or control of Greenland to the United States. The legal standing of this pact was questionable, as it was not formally sanctioned by the Danish state, with the Danish ambassador in Washington acting as a government in exile.
