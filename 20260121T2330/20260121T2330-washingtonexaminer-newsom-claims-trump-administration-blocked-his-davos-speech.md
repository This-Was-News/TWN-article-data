## Article summary
California Governor Gavin Newsom claimed on Wednesday that the Trump administration pressured the USA House at the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland, to cancel his scheduled speaking engagement. Newsom was slated to participate in a fireside chat at the USA House, which serves as the U.S. headquarters at the summit. The USA House reportedly denied him entry, citing that an elected official speaking did not align with their afternoon programming, though they offered him an evening reception. Newsom criticized this decision on X, calling the administration "weak and pathetic" for being scared of a fireside chat. A White House spokeswoman questioned Newsom's presence at the summit, calling him a "third-rate governor" who should focus on California's problems. Fortune, the media partner for the forum, confirmed Newsom's invitation but suggested his remarks were canceled due to logistical or security reasons involving multiple stakeholders. This incident occurred after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent mocked Newsom at a Davos event, calling him "economically illiterate Ken doll." Despite the friction, President Trump reportedly spoke kindly of Newsom during his own address, calling him a "good guy."
