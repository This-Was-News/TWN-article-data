## Article summary
Minnesota Governor Tim Walz and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey are reportedly under criminal investigation by the Department of Justice. Sources indicate the investigation stems from allegations that Walz and Frey impeded federal law enforcement's ability to operate in the state, particularly concerning Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) operations. This situation has intensified following a recent incident where an ICE officer shot and killed Renee Good, a woman who was allegedly obstructing traffic. Governor Walz publicly denounced the investigation as an attempt to intimidate him for his stance against the federal government's actions. Mayor Frey echoed these sentiments, stating his focus remains on public safety and that he will not be intimidated by what he perceives as a politically motivated investigation. The investigation is reportedly under a federal statute concerning conspiracy to impede a federal investigation. Minnesota's attorney general has also initiated a lawsuit against the federal government, asserting an unconstitutional federal presence in the state. Both Walz and Frey have been vocal critics of the increased federal law enforcement presence in Minnesota.
