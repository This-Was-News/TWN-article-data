## Article summary
A recent AP-NORC poll reveals that nearly a year into his second term, President Donald Trump's economic performance has not met the expectations of many within his own party. While a significant majority of Republicans (about 8 in 10) still approve of his job performance, there's a notable decline in their satisfaction with his handling of the economy compared to his first term. Specifically, only 16% of Republicans believe Trump has significantly helped address the cost of living, a sharp drop from 49% in April 2024. Similarly, satisfaction with job creation and healthcare cost reduction has decreased among his supporters. Despite economic concerns, Republicans remain largely supportive of Trump's immigration policies, with about 8 in 10 believing he has helped on immigration and border security. However, even on this issue, his approval among Republicans has seen a slight dip. The poll also indicates that while Republicans feel the country as a whole is improving, only about half believe their personal financial situations have gotten better. Many Republicans acknowledge Trump's personality flaws but prioritize his policy goals for the nation. The poll surveyed 1,203 adults, including 404 Republicans, and highlights a complex mix of satisfaction and lingering concerns among Trump's base.
