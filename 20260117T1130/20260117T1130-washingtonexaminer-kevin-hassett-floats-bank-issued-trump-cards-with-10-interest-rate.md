## Article summary
White House National Economic Council Director Kevin Hassett revealed that the Trump administration is in discussions with major banks regarding the voluntary issuance of "Trump cards." These cards would feature a 10% interest rate cap, aligning with President Donald Trump's recent call for such a measure to address affordability concerns. Instead of legislative action, the administration is exploring this voluntary approach with banks. Hassett explained that these cards would be targeted towards individuals with stable incomes who are currently underserved by traditional credit markets. This initiative is part of a broader affordability agenda the President plans to promote at the World Economic Forum. Other proposals include allowing the use of retirement funds for home down payments and a plan to significantly reduce prescription drug prices. The administration is also considering a tariff rebate check for lower- and middle-income households. While some lawmakers have proposed mandatory interest rate caps, industry groups have cautioned against such measures, warning they could increase costs for Americans.
