## Article summary
Utah County Attorney Jeff Gray testified Friday in the pretrial hearing for Tyler James Robinson, who is charged with the murder of conservative leader Charlie Kirk. Gray took the witness stand at the request of Robinson's defense attorney, Richard Novak, who is seeking to disqualify the prosecution team due to an alleged conflict of interest. The conflict arises because a prosecutor in Gray's office had a daughter who was present at the rally where Kirk was shot and killed at Utah Valley University. Gray testified that he and the prosecutor drove to the university after receiving a text from the prosecutor's daughter, who had already left the scene. He also stated that he did not establish a firewall between the prosecutor and the rest of the team. Gray emphasized that informing the defense was an act of caution, not an admission of conflict. He also mentioned that other individuals from his church ward had adult children at the event. Robinson faces charges including aggravated murder, witness tampering, and obstruction of justice, with Gray intending to seek the death penalty if convicted. The judge ordered that Robinson not be filmed during the proceedings to prevent lip-reading.
