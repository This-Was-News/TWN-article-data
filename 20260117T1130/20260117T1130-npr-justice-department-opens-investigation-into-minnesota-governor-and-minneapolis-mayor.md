## Article summary
The Justice Department has initiated an investigation into Minnesota Governor Tim Walz and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey, according to a U.S. official who remained anonymous. Mayor Frey expressed his belief that this investigation is an attempt to intimidate him for his stance on issues concerning Minneapolis, its law enforcement, and residents, particularly in relation to the current administration's impact on the city. Governor Walz, while not directly addressing the investigation, criticized the "weaponization of the justice system against your opponents" as an authoritarian tactic, referencing other political figures and questioning why the federal agent involved in the shooting of Renee Good is not being investigated. The killing of Renee Macklin Good by an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officer last week has intensified opposition to federal agents operating within Minnesota. Both Governor Walz and Mayor Frey, along with other Minnesota Democrats, have been critical of ICE's activities in the state.
