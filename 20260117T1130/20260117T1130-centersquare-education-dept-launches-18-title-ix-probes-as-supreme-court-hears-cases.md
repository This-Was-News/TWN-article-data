## Article summary
The U.S. Department of Education's Office for Civil Rights has initiated 18 investigations into public schools and state education departments concerning allegations of Title IX violations related to transgender athletes participating in girls' sports. These investigations coincide with the Supreme Court's oral arguments on transgender sports cases. The core of the complaints argues that policies allowing transgender students to compete in sports discriminate based on sex, thereby violating Title IX protections and jeopardizing the safety and equal opportunities for women in educational programs. Assistant Secretary for Civil Rights Kimberly Richey stated that the Office for Civil Rights is actively pursuing allegations of discrimination against women and girls by entities reportedly allowing males to compete in women's sports. The University of Nevada, Reno, one of the institutions under investigation, has stated they will respond appropriately and remain committed to an inclusive campus environment. The Department is also investigating the California Community College Athletic Association's policy on transgender participation. Experts argue that expanding Title IX to include transgender status has harmed the notion of women's equality. The investigations cover entities across California, Connecticut, Hawaii, Maine, Massachusetts, Nevada, New York, Pennsylvania, Vermont, and Washington.
