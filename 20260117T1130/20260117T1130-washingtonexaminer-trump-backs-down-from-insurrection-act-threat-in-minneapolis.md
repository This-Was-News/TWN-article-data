## Article summary
President Donald Trump announced on January 16, 2026, that he would not be using the Insurrection Act in Minneapolis, despite having threatened to do so the previous day. Trump stated that while he does not believe there is a current need for the act, he would use it if necessary, emphasizing its power. He also claimed the act had been used by a significant percentage of past presidents. The President's initial threat came in response to protests in Minneapolis following the death of Renee Good, who was shot by an immigration enforcement agent after striking an agent with her vehicle. Trump had posted on social media that if Minnesota's "corrupt politicians" did not stop "professional agitators and insurrectionists," he would invoke the Insurrection Act. Minnesota Governor Tim Walz urged Trump to lower the political temperature and appealed for peaceful protest. The Department of Homeland Security and ICE responded by blaming Governor Walz's rhetoric for the violence, with ICE stating, "The buck stops with you, Governor."
