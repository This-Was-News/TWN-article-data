## Article summary
The first quarter of fiscal year 2026 (October-December 2025) saw the lowest number of illegal border crossings in U.S. history, with a total of 91,603 encounters/apprehensions nationwide. This represents a 95% decrease compared to the first quarter average under the Biden administration, which reported record highs. Specifically, Border Patrol agents apprehended only 21,815 illegal border crossers at the southwest border during this period, a significant drop from previous years. Monthly figures for December 2025 also remained historically low, with 30,698 nationwide crossings, a stark contrast to the 370,883 reported in December 2024. Furthermore, no illegal border crossers were released into the country through parole programs in December 2025, a reversal from the Biden administration's policies. U.S. Department of Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem stated that America's borders are safer than ever due to President Trump's leadership and the efforts of DHS law enforcement. These numbers are a complete reversal from the Biden era, which saw an estimated 14 million illegal border crossers, including over two million 'gotaways.' The Trump administration has terminated or revamped Biden-era parole and visa programs and implemented new policies for expedited removal. The administration is also working to identify waste, fraud, and abuse in federal immigration programs.
