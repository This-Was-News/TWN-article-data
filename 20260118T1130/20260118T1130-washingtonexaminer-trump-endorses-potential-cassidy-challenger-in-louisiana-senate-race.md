## Article summary
President Donald Trump has endorsed Representative Julia Letlow (R-LA) for the Senate seat currently held by Senator Bill Cassidy (R-LA). Trump praised Letlow as a "Great Star" and "TOTAL WINNER" on Truth Social, urging her to enter the race. Letlow responded by expressing honor for Trump's endorsement and stating her commitment to conservative leadership for Louisiana. Trump's support for Letlow comes as she was reportedly waiting for his backing before deciding to run. If Letlow enters the race, she would face Cassidy in a potentially bruising Republican primary. Cassidy, seeking a third term, has a significant campaign fund and the support of Senate Majority Leader John Thune. Trump's endorsement is seen as potentially pivotal in the primary. The endorsement also highlights Trump's dissatisfaction with Cassidy, who voted to convict Trump during his impeachment trial and has criticized HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. Despite Trump's backing of Letlow, Cassidy expressed confidence in his ability to win re-election.
