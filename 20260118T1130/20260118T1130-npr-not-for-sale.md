## Article summary
Thousands of people protested in Copenhagen against President Trump's expressed desire to acquire Greenland. Demonstrators marched from City Hall to the U.S. embassy, waving Greenlandic flags and chanting "Greenland is not for sale." Many protesters wore red hats modified with the slogan "Make America go away," directly opposing Trump's "Make America great again" merchandise. The protest occurred shortly after a bipartisan Congressional delegation visited Copenhagen to reassure Danish and Greenlandic officials about the U.S. relationship, aiming to counter Trump's rhetoric. Attendees expressed deep disappointment and resentment towards the U.S. for what they perceive as a disrespectful and offensive proposal. Some protesters highlighted historical instances of land acquisition by the U.S. and emphasized that Greenland and its people are not for sale. Concerns were raised about the emotional and political uncertainty created by Trump's statements, with some fearing a fundamental reshaping of the world order and the potential collapse of NATO if such an acquisition were pursued. Protesters urged American citizens to contact their representatives to preserve NATO and respect Greenland's sovereignty.
