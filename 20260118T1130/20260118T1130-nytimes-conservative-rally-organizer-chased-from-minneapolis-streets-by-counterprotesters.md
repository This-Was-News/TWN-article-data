## Article summary
A protest organized by conservative influencer Jake Lang in Minneapolis was met with a larger counterprotest, leading to scuffles and taunts. Lang, who was pardoned after the Jan. 6 riot, was chased into a hotel lobby by counterprotesters, with water balloons being thrown at him in cold weather. Police responded to the scene. The counterprotest, led by The Peoples Action Coalition Against Trump, significantly outnumbered Lang's group, the March Against Minnesota Fraud. Counterprotesters also demonstrated against Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), playing music from "Frozen." The event lasted about two hours and drew hundreds of attendees. The protests occurred amid heightened ICE activity in the Twin Cities following the shooting death of Renee Good by an immigration agent. Lang's protest targeted an investigation into Minnesota's social services programs, where most charged individuals have been of Somali origin, with billions in taxpayer funds suspected of being stolen. A federal judge had recently issued restrictions on ICE agents' actions toward protesters in the state.
