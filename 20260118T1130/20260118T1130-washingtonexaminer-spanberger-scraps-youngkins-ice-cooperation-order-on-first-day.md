## Article summary
Virginia Governor Abigail Spanberger has rescinded an executive order by former Governor Glenn Youngkin that mandated cooperation between state law enforcement and U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). Spanberger stated that the order was an inappropriate use of state resources, arguing that it diverted police from their core public safety duties. She believes that federal authorities should handle federal immigration laws, while state and local law enforcement should focus on enforcing state laws and ensuring the safety of all Virginia residents. This decision aligns with Spanberger's stated intention to resist the Trump administration's aggressive deportation policies. She emphasized in her inaugural address that law-abiding immigrants are included in her commitment to the security and safety of all Virginians. The move follows scrutiny of ICE tactics after a fatal shooting in Minnesota, where Democratic lawmakers had threatened to cut off cooperation. The Department of Justice has initiated a criminal inquiry into Minnesota Governor Tim Walz and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey, accusing them of impeding federal law enforcement.
