## Article summary
Governor Tim Walz has mobilized the Minnesota National Guard in response to anti-ICE protests across the state. The troops are on standby, ready to assist local law enforcement and ensure public safety while protecting citizens' First Amendment rights. These demonstrations follow heightened tensions after a fatal shooting in early January and other rallies, including one addressing daycare fraud. Some protests have seen clashes between anti-ICE demonstrators and right-wing activists, with one activist claiming to have been stabbed. Despite these incidents, the police department reported that protesters were generally compliant with dispersal orders. Notably, Governor Walz and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey are under federal investigation by the Department of Justice for allegedly impeding federal law enforcement's operations in the state, stemming from their public statements critical of ICE.
