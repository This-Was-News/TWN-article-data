## Article summary
Hundreds of counterprotesters gathered in Minneapolis to oppose a conservative influencer's pro-immigration crackdown rally. The counterprotesters significantly outnumbered the supporters of Jake Lang, who organized the demonstration. Lang, a conservative influencer and Senate candidate, appeared to sustain injuries as he was chased away from the scene by counterprotesters. The event occurred amidst increased immigration enforcement in the Twin Cities, involving over 2,000 federal officers. Protests have been ongoing since the Department of Homeland Security intensified its operations. A federal judge recently ruled that immigration officers cannot detain or use tear gas on peaceful protesters not obstructing authorities. The article also highlights the fear experienced by some immigrants, including Garrison Gibson, who fled Liberia and expressed apprehension about leaving his home after being detained. His recent re-arrest, which his family attributed to White House Deputy Chief of Staff Stephen Miller, was denied by the White House. The National Guard was mobilized by Governor Tim Walz to support law enforcement but had not yet been deployed to the streets.
