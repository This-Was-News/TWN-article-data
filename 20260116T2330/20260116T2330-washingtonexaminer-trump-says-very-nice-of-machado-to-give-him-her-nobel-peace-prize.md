## Article summary
President Donald Trump stated that Venezuelan opposition leader María Corina Machado "very nicely" presented him with her Nobel Peace Prize during a White House meeting. Machado reportedly told Trump that he deserved the prize for ending wars. Trump expressed admiration for Machado, calling her a "very fine woman," though he also indicated a preference for Venezuela's acting President Delcy Rodríguez to lead the country in the absence of Nicolás Maduro, citing past negative outcomes when leadership changes occurred, such as in Iraq. The White House shared an image of the meeting, with Machado stating the award was in recognition of Trump's commitment to freedom. Additionally, Trump dismissed reports that Israel and Arab leaders had urged him not to strike Iran, claiming he convinced himself to delay action due to a halt in executions. He also downplayed the possibility of invoking the Insurrection Act to quell protests in Minneapolis, stating he would use it if necessary but saw no current need.
