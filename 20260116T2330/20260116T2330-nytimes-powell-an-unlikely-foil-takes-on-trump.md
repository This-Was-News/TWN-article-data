## Article summary
Jerome H. Powell, the Federal Reserve chair, has unexpectedly become a foil to President Trump, largely due to his significant popularity in Washington and beyond. This popularity has bolstered support for Powell and the independence of the Federal Reserve, especially after the Justice Department launched a criminal investigation into him. Powell had proactively built relationships with members of Congress, a strategy that proved invaluable when he confronted the Trump administration. Many Republican lawmakers, who had previously remained silent on Trump's attacks on the Fed, strongly criticized the investigation as an overreach. This bipartisan backing extended to former Fed chairs, Treasury secretaries from both parties, and even Wall Street leaders. Powell's approach to his role, characterized by years of deliberate relationship-building on Capitol Hill and clear communication with the public, has earned him respect across the political spectrum. Despite facing criticism for initial missteps in addressing inflation and other crises, Powell is often lauded for his efforts to stabilize the economy. As his term as chair concludes, his potential continued role as a governor is now seen as crucial for safeguarding the Fed's independence against political pressure. Powell's commitment to public service and the institution's integrity suggests he will stand firm against threats, even if it means remaining in a position that could impede the president's agenda.
