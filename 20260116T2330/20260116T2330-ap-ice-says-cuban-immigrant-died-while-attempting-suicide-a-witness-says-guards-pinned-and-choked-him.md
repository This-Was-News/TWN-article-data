## Article summary
A Cuban immigrant named Geraldo Lunas Campos died on January 3, 2026, at a Texas immigration detention facility. The federal government claims he died during a suicide attempt where staff tried to save him. However, a witness stated that guards choked him after he was handcuffed and tackled, leading to his death. The El Paso County Medical Examiners Office informed the family that a preliminary autopsy suggests the death was a homicide due to asphyxia from chest and neck compression. This incident has increased scrutiny on immigration jail conditions, especially as the government detains large numbers of immigrants. ICE initially reported Campos' death without mentioning an altercation, but later amended their account to include his alleged suicide attempt and resistance to staff. A detainee witness contradicted the official account, stating Campos was handcuffed and choked by multiple guards, and that he could not breathe. Camp Montana East, where Campos died, is a large tent facility built by a contractor with no prior experience in running a corrections facility. Campos, a registered sex offender, had a history of criminal convictions. His daughter disputes the child sexual abuse accusation, calling it false and part of a custody battle. Conflicting accounts persist, with ICE stating Campos became disruptive and resisted staff, while the witness insists he was subdued by force. The investigation is ongoing, and the final determination of homicide by the medical examiner could impact legal liability. The family is seeking justice and the return of Campos' body.
