## Article summary
President Donald Trump has decided not to invoke the Insurrection Act in response to unrest in Minneapolis, despite his earlier threat. Trump stated that while he believes the act is powerful and would use it if necessary, there is currently no need for it. He had previously warned that he would deploy the Insurrection Act if Minnesota's "corrupt politicians" did not stop "professional agitators and insurrectionists" from attacking ICE agents. The unrest in Minneapolis was sparked by the death of Renee Good, who was shot by an immigration enforcement agent after striking the agent with her vehicle. Minnesota Governor Tim Walz urged Trump to "turn the temperature down" and stop what he called a "campaign of retribution." In response, ICE and DHS blamed the governor's rhetoric for the violence, with ICE stating that "the buck stops with you, Governor."
