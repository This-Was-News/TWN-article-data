## Article summary
A new Republican-led congressional subcommittee investigating the Jan. 6, 2021, attack on the U.S. Capitol has been criticized for promoting false and misleading claims. During its first hearing, GOP lawmakers focused on the pipe bomb incidents outside the Democratic and Republican national committees, aiming to scrutinize the Biden administration's handling of the investigation. This hearing occurred amidst a broader effort by the Trump administration to reframe the narrative of the Jan. 6 attack. NPR fact-checked several claims made during the hearing, including accusations that the Biden FBI entrapped MAGA supporters. The fact check revealed that Joe Biden was not president at the time of the attack, and the FBI, led by a Trump appointee, found no evidence of undercover agents encouraging illegal acts. Another point of contention was the deployment of the National Guard, with Republican lawmakers suggesting delays were due to requests from the legislative branch. However, reports indicate the Capitol Police requested assistance prior to the building breach, and the president has direct control over the D.C. National Guard. The hearing also saw criticism of the testimonies of Capitol Police officers, with claims of them being 'Trump haters' with 'pre-scripted' testimonies. This was countered by evidence that some officers, like Michael Fanone, had previously voted for Trump but later regretted their decision due to their experiences on Jan. 6.
