## Article summary
Venezuelan opposition leader Maria Corina Machado presented President Donald Trump with a Nobel Peace Prize medal during a private lunch. Machado stated the gesture symbolized a historical parallel to a medal gifted by General Lafayette to Simón Bolívar, representing brotherhood and the fight for freedom. While Trump has publicly expressed a desire for the Nobel Prize, the Nobel Committee has clarified that the award cannot be revoked or transferred. This event follows Trump's previous skepticism about Machado's leadership in Venezuela. Despite this, Machado expressed confidence in Trump's support for Venezuela's freedom after their meeting. The U.S. has recently taken actions impacting Venezuela's oil industry, including seizing an oil tanker. Machado's visit to Washington also included meetings with U.S. lawmakers and a prior meeting with Pope Leo XIV. White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt indicated that Trump's meeting with Machado was expected to be a positive discussion about the situation in Venezuela, without expecting her to commit him to an election timeline.
