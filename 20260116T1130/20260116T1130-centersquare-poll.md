## Article summary
A recent poll by Noble Predictive Insights reveals a significant 16-percentage-point decline in President Donald Trump's approval rating among Arizonans between February and December, dropping from +3% to -13%. This decrease is attributed to "pocketbook issues" and policy uncertainties, according to CEO Mike Noble.  The poll also indicates that Democratic Governor Katie Hobbs' approval rating has fallen from +6% to +1% over the same period.  Inflation, affordable housing, and healthcare are the top concerns for Arizonans, with immigration's importance diminishing.  Overall, 52% of Arizonans believe the state is heading in the wrong direction, a sentiment linked to the rising cost of living.  The upcoming gubernatorial race in Arizona is expected to be highly competitive, with Governor Hobbs in a statistical tie with leading Republican candidates, Andy Biggs and Karrin Taylor Robson, according to an Emerson College poll.  The Arizona gubernatorial race is currently rated as a "toss up" by Cook Political Report.
