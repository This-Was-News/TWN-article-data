## Article summary
South Korea's former president, Yoon Suk Yeol, has been sentenced to five years in prison for declaring martial law on December 3, 2024. The Seoul Central District Court found him guilty of charges including fabricating official documents, failing to follow legal procedures for martial law, and obstructing his arrest. The judge stated that Yoon's actions plunged the country into a political crisis and criticized his lack of remorse. This is the first of several verdicts expected, with Yoon still facing a trial for insurrection, where prosecutors may seek the death penalty. He also faces charges related to violating cabinet members' rights and blocking investigators. Yoon, who won the presidency in March 2022, declared martial law which was met with strong opposition and unanimously rebuked by the National Assembly. He retracted the order and apologized, but faced impeachment motions. An arrest warrant was issued on December 31, 2024, and he was arrested on January 15, 2025. His impeachment was finalized on April 4, 2025, removing him from office.
