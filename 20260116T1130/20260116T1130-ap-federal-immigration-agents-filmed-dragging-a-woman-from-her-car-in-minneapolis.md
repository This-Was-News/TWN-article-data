## Article summary
A video has surfaced showing federal immigration agents dragging a U.S. citizen, Aliya Rahman, out of her car in Minneapolis. Rahman stated she was on her way to a medical appointment when she encountered agents and was subsequently detained, alleging she was denied medical care and lost consciousness. The Department of Homeland Security described Rahman as an agitator who obstructed their agents. This incident is part of a larger pattern of intensified immigration crackdowns in Minneapolis, with numerous viral videos documenting confrontations between federal agents and civilians. Other videos show agents using battering rams to enter homes and detaining U.S. citizens. Rahman's attorney claims she was in a difficult position, with no safe way to move her vehicle. The Department of Homeland Security has not specified if Rahman faces charges but confirmed her arrest along with six others deemed agitators. Rahman was treated for injuries consistent with assault and has since been released from the hospital.
