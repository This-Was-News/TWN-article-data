## Article summary
Emergency call transcripts and police reports detail the unfolding crisis on January 7th when Renee Good was shot by an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officer in Minneapolis. The incident occurred amidst a confrontation between federal agents and observers, with multiple witnesses immediately calling 911. Transcripts reveal the confusion and panic of bystanders, describing the shooting and Ms. Good's attempts to drive away before crashing her vehicle. Paramedics arrived within minutes to find Ms. Good unresponsive with multiple gunshot wounds. Despite resuscitation efforts en route to the hospital, she was pronounced dead. The Minneapolis Police Department reports indicate the ICE agent involved remained on the scene. Incident reports highlight the escalating tension, with police needing to manage a hostile crowd and block off the area. Communications show attempts to contact the federal agents in charge to have them leave the scene, which proved difficult due to the agitated crowd. Eventually, all ICE agents departed, and the crowd calmed down. Within an hour of the shooting, videos of the event began circulating globally.
