## Article summary
The 2025 National Hockey League Stanley Cup champions, the Florida Panthers, were honored at the White House by President Donald Trump. This marks the second consecutive year the Panthers have won the championship and received this recognition. Trump praised the team's dominance, highlighting their first-ever Presidents' Trophy, three straight Finals appearances, and two consecutive Stanley Cup victories. He playfully teased the team about the one Final they didn't win. The Panthers presented Trump with a No. 47 jersey, championship rings, and a golden hockey stick. Team owner Vincent Viola expressed his gratitude for the President's kindness and the honor of being at the White House. Star player Matthew Tkachuk also spoke, expressing pride and the team's hope to return next year.
