## Article summary
Conn Selmer, the largest manufacturer of band instruments in the United States, is planning to close its Eastlake, Ohio plant by June 30. The company intends to move some production overseas and transfer other manufacturing to its Elkhart, Indiana facility. This proposed closure is subject to negotiations with the union representing the plant's hourly employees. The Eastlake factory's closure would result in the loss of 150 jobs, a significant blow to the community of about 18,000. Mayor Kevin Castell expressed disappointment and hopes for a resolution that could keep the plant in Eastlake. Conn Selmer stated that the decision aims to improve efficiency, competitiveness, and meet market demands by consolidating professional brass manufacturing in Elkhart and percussion manufacturing in Monroe, North Carolina. The company emphasized its long-standing commitment to U.S. manufacturing over its 150-year history.
