## Article summary
President Donald Trump held a nearly two-hour press briefing to mark his first year in office, expressing frustration that voters aren't appreciating his economic achievements. The briefing, which largely resembled a campaign rally, featured Trump displaying mugshots of arrested immigrants and a large binder of his administration's accomplishments. He acknowledged that his administration, and perhaps he himself, has struggled with public relations in communicating their successes, suggesting "fake news" and a lack of promotion are contributing factors. Trump voiced concern that the Supreme Court might strike down his tariffs, warning that without them, China would dominate economically and the U.S. would lose leverage with European nations. He also reiterated his desire to acquire Greenland, hinting at potential actions if negotiations fail and suggesting he could use "other alternatives" if tariffs are removed. Regarding NATO, Trump claimed he has strengthened the alliance and expressed optimism about reaching an agreement for Greenland's acquisition. Finally, while highlighting ICE's operations, Trump conceded that the agency sometimes makes "rough" mistakes or errors, even mentioning the recent killing of Renee Good by an ICE officer, an incident he found "horrible."
