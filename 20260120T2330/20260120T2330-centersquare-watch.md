## Article summary
President Donald Trump announced his intention to issue $2,000 tariff rebate checks to certain Americans without congressional approval, a departure from previous statements by his economic adviser. Trump expressed confidence that this could be achieved using tariff revenue, which he also plans to use to reduce the national debt. Details of the plan, including income limits for recipients, remain unspecified, though suggestions have ranged up to $100,000 annually. The cost of such a program is estimated to be substantial, potentially exceeding expected tariff revenue, according to analyses by the Tax Foundation and the Committee for a Responsible Federal Budget. Furthermore, a recent report indicates that Americans, not foreign nations, are bearing the brunt of Trump's tariffs. The legality of Trump's tariffs is currently under review by the Supreme Court, with a decision expected by June.
