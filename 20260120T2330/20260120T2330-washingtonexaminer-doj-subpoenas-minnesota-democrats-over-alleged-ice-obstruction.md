## Article summary
The Department of Justice has served grand jury subpoenas to several top Minnesota government officials, including Governor Tim Walz, Attorney General Keith Ellison, and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey, as part of a federal investigation into alleged obstruction of U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) operations. Governor Walz publicly acknowledged the investigation, accusing the Trump administration of using the Justice Department to target political opponents and calling the probe a partisan distraction. The subpoenas reportedly seek records and communications related to alleged interference with federal immigration enforcement. This investigation follows weeks of tension between the Trump administration and Minnesota Democrats over responses to stepped-up federal immigration actions. Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche had previously warned that public statements by Walz and Frey were approaching potential criminal conduct. Both Walz and Frey have defiantly pushed back, framing the DOJ investigation as an attempt to intimidate them for standing up for their communities. Attorney General Keith Ellison announced he is staying in his race for reelection, stating he is best equipped to defend Minnesotans from the federal government. The investigation appears to stem from public confrontations by Minnesota officials against the Trump administration regarding ICE operations, which have also led to protests.
