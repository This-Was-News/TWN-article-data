## Article summary
President Trump and his administration are displaying contempt for European leaders ahead of his visit to Europe and the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent mocked European leaders' efforts to block Trump's potential acquisition of Greenland, sarcastically referring to their actions as the 'dreaded European working group.' The administration views Europe as a collection of weak, liberal-dominated nations hindered by bureaucracy, with the national security strategy criticizing Europe's 'civilizational self-confidence' and 'regulatory suffocation.' Trump himself posted an AI-generated meme suggesting Greenland would become a U.S. territory by 2026, even before arriving in Switzerland. As he prepared to speak at Davos, Trump continued to express dismissive scorn towards the leaders he was about to meet, highlighting a pattern of overt mockery from his administration towards Europe.
