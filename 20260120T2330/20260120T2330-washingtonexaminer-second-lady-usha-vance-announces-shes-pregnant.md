## Article summary
Second Lady Usha Vance and Vice President JD Vance are expecting their fourth child, a baby boy, due in July. The couple shared their exciting news via an Instagram post, stating that Usha and the baby are doing well. They are already parents to Ewan, Vivek, and Mirabel. The Vances expressed gratitude to the military doctors and staff for their support during this "exciting and hectic time," enabling them to serve the country while maintaining a family life. This pregnancy marks a historic moment, as Usha Vance will be the first sitting second lady to give birth while in office. While unprecedented for a second lady, first ladies have given birth while in the White House, including Frances Cleveland and Jacqueline Kennedy. The Vances join other members of the Trump administration who are expecting, with press secretary Karoline Leavitt also announcing a pregnancy in December 2025.
