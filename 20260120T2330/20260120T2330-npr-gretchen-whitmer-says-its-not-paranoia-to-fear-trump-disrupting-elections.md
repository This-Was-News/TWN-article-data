## Article summary
Michigan Governor Gretchen Whitmer expressed concerns about potential disruptions to upcoming elections by the Trump administration, stating it's not "paranoia" to anticipate such actions. She revealed that Democratic governors are preparing for these possibilities through "tabletop" exercises. Whitmer also criticized the "terrible toll" of Trump's tariffs on the U.S. auto industry, arguing they lead to job losses despite some union leadership support. She highlighted a concerning trend of men falling behind economically and expressed a desire to understand why the Democratic party is losing support among this demographic. Whitmer believes that America is ready to elect a woman president, disagreeing with Michelle Obama's assessment based on recent election results. She suggested that Kamala Harris's past defeat was due to multiple factors beyond just gender. The governor, in her final year as governor, is focused on electing other Democrats and has not made moves toward a presidential run in 2028. The interview took place at the Detroit Auto Show, a venue that also served as a ballot-counting site in 2020 amidst pro-Trump protests.
