## Article summary
President Trump has criticized the United Kingdom's decision to cede sovereignty of the Chagos Islands to Mauritius, calling it an act of "great stupidity." He suggested this move is a primary reason for his desire to acquire Greenland, a territory controlled by Denmark. The agreement allows the U.K. to continue leasing the key U.S.-U.K. military base at Diego Garcia for 99 years. Despite previous U.S. support for the deal, Trump expressed concern that China and Russia would perceive it as a sign of weakness. The British Prime Minister's office stated the deal was necessary to protect the base, which was under threat due to court decisions. They emphasized that the agreement secures the base's operations for generations and has been welcomed by U.S. and other Five Eyes allies. U.K. officials expressed surprise at Trump's comments and plan to discuss the matter with the U.S. administration. U.S. House Speaker Mike Johnson, visiting the U.K., acknowledged the tensions and stated his mission was to help calm the waters between the two nations.
