## Article summary
Special prosecutor Jack Smith defended his investigation into Donald Trump in a House hearing, stating that no one is above the law and Trump's actions necessitated accountability. Smith asserted that Trump engaged in criminal activity that jeopardized democracy and the rule of law, framing his testimony as a summation he couldn't deliver in court. The hearing presented risks for Smith, as House Republicans threatened a criminal referral if inconsistencies arose. Trump publicly criticized Smith on Truth Social, urging the Attorney General to investigate Smith's conduct and witnesses used in the case against him. Smith's testimony aimed to justify his actions in indicting Trump twice, emphasizing the legal requirements to hold him accountable for undermining democratic processes.
