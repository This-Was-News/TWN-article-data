## Article summary
Senator Amy Klobuchar has filed paperwork for a potential run for Minnesota governor following Governor Tim Walz's unexpected withdrawal from the reelection campaign. Walz's decision to exit the race came amidst federal investigations into his administration's handling of alleged fraud in the state's social services programs. Klobuchar, a prominent figure in the Democratic party, reportedly received encouragement to run and met with Walz before his announcement. Democratic strategists are reportedly enthusiastic about her possible candidacy. If Klobuchar enters and wins the gubernatorial race, it would mean cutting short her current Senate term. She is a well-known figure and is expected to lead the Democratic primary field, especially after Attorney General Keith Ellison opted to seek reelection instead of running for governor. The Republican primary field includes candidates such as MyPillow CEO Mike Lindell and Minnesota House Speaker Lisa Demuth, with Kendall Qualls appearing to lead the GOP primary based on a recent straw poll. The filing of Klobuchar's paperwork suggests an imminent official announcement of her gubernatorial bid.
