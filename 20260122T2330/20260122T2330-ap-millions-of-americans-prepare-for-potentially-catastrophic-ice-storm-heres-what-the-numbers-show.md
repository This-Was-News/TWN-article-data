## Article summary
Millions of Americans are preparing for a potentially catastrophic ice storm that is expected to bring damaging ice, heavy snow, and freezing rain across a vast area of the United States. The storm is projected to impact regions from New Mexico to the Carolinas, with major cities like Washington D.C., Philadelphia, New York City, and Boston facing significant snowfall. Forecasters warn that ice accumulation of half an inch or more could lead to widespread and long-lasting power outages by toppling trees and power lines. Approximately 160 million people, nearly half of the U.S. population, are under various winter storm watches and warnings. Major U.S. hub airports in the storm's path, including Dallas-Fort Worth, Atlanta, Memphis, and Charlotte, are anticipating delays for both passengers and cargo. States and cities are mobilizing resources, with many having substantial amounts of salt and snow-removal equipment ready. For instance, Arkansas has over 78,000 cubic yards of salt on hand, and Texas possesses more than 1,000 pieces of winter weather equipment. Some cities are employing innovative methods, like Chicago using beet juice to lower the freezing point of water on roads. In contrast, some areas, like Jackson, Mississippi, have no dedicated snowplows, relying on other heavy machinery. Meanwhile, parts of the Northern Plains could experience extreme wind chills as low as 50 degrees below zero. The storm is expected to begin Friday and continue through the weekend, with an atmospheric river of moisture potentially exacerbating precipitation along the Gulf Coast and moving northeast.
