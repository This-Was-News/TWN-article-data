## Article summary
Vice President J.D. Vance visited Minneapolis to address ongoing unrest and defend U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). During a news conference, Vance criticized the mainstream media for what he described as lies about ICE officials and protestors, stating that ICE personnel are under "incredible amount of duress and chaos." He emphasized the need to "tone down the temperature" and "reduce the chaos" while upholding the right to peaceful protest and the enforcement of federal immigration laws. Vance promised repercussions for violence, stating that the Trump Department of Justice will prosecute those who assault law enforcement officers. This visit followed federal arrests in connection with a protest that disrupted a St. Paul church service, an event organized in part by Black Lives Matter Minnesota. The protest escalated after the Jan. 7 killing of Renee Nicole Good during an encounter with ICE officers. Vance attributed the chaos in Minneapolis to a lack of cooperation between local and federal authorities, noting that Minneapolis's status as a sanctuary city prevents law enforcement from cooperating with federal officials on immigration matters.
