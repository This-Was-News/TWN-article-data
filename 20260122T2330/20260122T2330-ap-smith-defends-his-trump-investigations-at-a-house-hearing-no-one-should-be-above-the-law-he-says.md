## Article summary
Former Justice Department special counsel Jack Smith defended his investigations into Donald Trump during a public congressional hearing, asserting that no one is above the law and that his actions were driven by evidence, not politics. He stated that his team developed proof beyond a reasonable doubt of Trump's criminal activity in the cases concerning the 2020 election and the handling of classified documents. Republican lawmakers, led by committee chairman Rep. Jim Jordan, accused Smith of political motivation and overreach, particularly regarding the subpoenaing of phone records of GOP members. Democrats, however, supported Smith, emphasizing the rule of law. Smith described a wide-ranging conspiracy to overturn the election, highlighting that key witnesses were Trump's own Republican supporters. He also addressed concerns about the Justice Department's independence, stating that accountability is crucial for protecting democracy. Republicans countered that Smith's investigations were intended to derail Trump's presidential bid, while Smith maintained that Trump was the most culpable in the conspiracy to undo the election results.
