## Article summary
Donald Trump's first year in office has seen the implementation of many policies from Project 2025, a comprehensive policy plan developed by the Heritage Foundation and other conservative organizations. Despite initially claiming ignorance about the project, numerous initiatives outlined in the 900-page blueprint have been enacted. These include a crackdown on immigration, the dismantling of the Department of Education, and the termination of diversity, equity, and inclusion programs. President Trump also declared that there are only two genders, male and female, as official U.S. government policy. Legal challenges have been mounted by state attorneys general, who view Project 2025 as a clear roadmap of the administration's intentions. While the White House dismisses these concerns as irrelevant, supporters of Project 2025 see the rollout of these policies as an endorsement of their work. The controversy surrounding Project 2025 was amplified when Trump distanced himself from it during his campaign, which some believe made it more politically potent. Key figures involved in Project 2025, such as Russell Vought, have been appointed to significant positions within the administration, further integrating the plan into government operations. The implementation of these policies has been described as defining Trump's first year back in office.
