## Article summary
The Sundance Film Festival is preparing for its final edition in Park City, Utah, marking a period of significant transition. This year's festival will bid farewell to its longtime home and its founder, Robert Redford, who passed away in September. Next year, the festival will relocate to Boulder, Colorado. Despite these changes, the festival will celebrate Redford's legacy with screenings of his films and tributes at fundraising events. The event will feature a diverse lineup of independent films, including star-studded features and thought-provoking documentaries. Notable films include Cathy Yan's satire 'The Gallerist' starring Natalie Portman and Gregg Araki's 'I Want Your Sex' featuring Olivia Wilde. Documentaries will cover subjects ranging from celebrities like Brittney Griner and Salman Rushdie to urgent global issues. Many filmmakers, including Gregg Araki and Jay Duplass, express a sense of bittersweet nostalgia for Park City but are optimistic about Sundance's future. They emphasize that the festival's core mission of fostering independent cinema will endure regardless of location. The event will showcase emerging talent and established artists, continuing its role as a vital platform for independent filmmaking.
