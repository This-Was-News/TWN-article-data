## Article summary
Federal Reserve Chairman Jerome Powell is set to attend a Supreme Court hearing concerning President Trump's authority to remove a Fed governor. This comes amidst a DOJ criminal investigation into Powell, which he claims is an attempt to influence monetary policy. The case involves Fed Governor Lisa Cook, whom Trump seeks to fire for alleged mortgage fraud. Trump's legal team argues the central bank's role in the economy necessitates removing ethically compromised members. Powell has publicly stated the DOJ investigation is a pretext to pressure him into lowering interest rates, thus threatening the Fed's independence. Trump has consistently urged the Fed to cut rates, believing it would stimulate the economy, while the Fed maintains that premature rate cuts could lead to inflation. Powell's attendance at the hearing may signal continued resistance to presidential pressure on the central bank.
