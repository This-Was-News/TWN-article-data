## Article summary
The 2026 Winter Olympics in Milan and Cortina, Italy, will be the most geographically widespread in history, utilizing 25 venues across four clusters: Milan, Cortina d'Ampezzo, Valtellina, and Val di Fiemme. This approach maximizes existing facilities to minimize environmental impact. Milan will host the opening ceremony at the San Siro Stadium, along with figure skating, short track, ice hockey, and speed skating events. Cortina d'Ampezzo, a returning host from 1956, will feature women's Alpine skiing, curling, and sliding sports like bobsled, luge, and skeleton. The Valtellina cluster, including Bormio and Livigno, will host men's Alpine skiing, ski mountaineering, freestyle skiing, and snowboarding. Val di Fiemme will be the site for cross-country skiing, ski jumping, and Nordic combined events. The closing ceremony is scheduled to take place in Verona's historic Roman amphitheater, the Verona Olympic Arena. Athletes will reside in six Olympic Villages spread throughout the region. Spectators are advised to plan for significant travel time between venues due to the extensive geographical spread.
