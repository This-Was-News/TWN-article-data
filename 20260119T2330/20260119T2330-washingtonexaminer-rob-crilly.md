## Article summary
Mike Pence is positioning himself to lead a resurgence of traditional conservatism, distinct from the populist movement associated with Donald Trump. His think tank, Advancing American Freedom (AAF), has seen a significant influx of staff, many from the Heritage Foundation, following a dispute over antisemitism and comments made by Tucker Carlson. This exodus from Heritage, triggered by its president's initial defense of Carlson's interview with Holocaust denier Nick Fuentes, has created a need for larger office space for Pence's organization. Pence believes this internal conservative debate offers an opportunity for principles like the right to life and Reaganite foreign policy to regain prominence. Despite criticism from MAGA loyalists for his role in certifying the 2020 election, Pence's influence appears to be growing, with increased media appearances and a teaching role at George Mason University. His chief of staff is actively seeking more office space to accommodate the expanding team, emphasizing the mission to counter 'big government populism.' Pence remains a prominent figure, planning to host a reception for the March for Life, an event Trump will also address via video. The article also touches on the UK's message to Europe to remain calm regarding Trump's actions on Greenland, and notes the White House's strategy of engaging with alternative media outlets to mark the anniversary of Trump's second inauguration.
