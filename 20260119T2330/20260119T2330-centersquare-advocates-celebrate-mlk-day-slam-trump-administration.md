## Article summary
Community leaders across the nation gathered to celebrate the life and legacy of Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. on Monday, with some using the occasion to criticize the Trump administration. Celebrations included peace walks, parades, health fairs, and events at museums and churches. In Atlanta, Bernice King, daughter of Martin Luther King Jr., spoke at Ebenezer Baptist Church, criticizing U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement measures and the Trump administration's efforts to restrict diversity, equity, and inclusion programs. She compared immigration enforcement to retaliation against civil rights protestors and stated that such actions would not deter their efforts to protest for rights. King also addressed Trump's comments about the Civil Rights Act of 1964, asserting that it was meant to heal the nation, not harm white people. In New York City, House Democratic Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries criticized the president during an MLK Day event. Parades and celebrations also took place in Los Angeles and Dallas, emphasizing unity, love, and determination in honoring Dr. King's dream.
