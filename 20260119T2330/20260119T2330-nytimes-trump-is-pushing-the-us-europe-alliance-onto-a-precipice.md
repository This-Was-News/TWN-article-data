## Article summary
The United States' alliance with Europe is being strained by President Trump's campaign to acquire Greenland against the wishes of its inhabitants. This situation raises concerns about whether resisting Trump's territorial ambitions could irreparably damage the relationship between Europe and the U.S. Some European leaders, like France's President Macron and Germany's finance minister, are considering strong economic measures in response to Trump's tariff threats. European leaders are set to convene in Brussels to formulate a unified response to these provocations. Observers note that the post-World War II alliance has shifted from one advancing democratic interests to a relationship dictated by Trump's terms, leveraging American power for his demands. The use of economic warfare against allies in this manner is considered unprecedented. The core question for European capitals is whether this alliance, now fundamentally altered, is doomed.
