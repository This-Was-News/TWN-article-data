## Article summary
Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem criticized CBS News's "Face the Nation" host Margaret Brennan for naming Immigration and Customs Enforcement officer Jonathan Ross, who was involved in a fatal shooting on January 7th. Renee Good, 37, was killed by Ross after she allegedly used her vehicle as a weapon against him. Noem defended Ross, stating he acted based on his training during a dangerous encounter where his life was threatened. She argued against publicizing his name, citing an 8,000% increase in death threats against law enforcement and concerns for Ross and his family's safety. Despite Brennan pointing out that Ross's name was public, Noem insisted it should not continue to be shared. The Department of Homeland Security confirmed that Ross suffered internal injuries during the incident and is recovering. The department is following its standard investigative procedures, but it remains unclear if Ross has returned to duty. The incident highlights a significant surge in assaults against ICE officers, with a reported 1,153% increase in 11 months. Ross had also been involved in a prior vehicle ramming incident.
