## Article summary
Former President Donald Trump has endorsed Representative Julia Letlow in her potential challenge against incumbent Senator Bill Cassidy of Louisiana, a Republican who voted to convict Trump in his second impeachment trial. Trump announced his support for Letlow on Truth Social, urging her to run and promising his full backing. This move is expected to create new tensions within the Republican party, as Senate leadership, including Majority Leader John Thune, had urged Trump to support Cassidy, citing his crucial role on the Senate's health committee. Senator Cassidy responded confidently, stating he is proudly running for re-election and is confident he will win if Letlow enters the race. Letlow expressed honor at the endorsement and indicated a likely campaign announcement, emphasizing the need for unwavering conservative leadership. Trump's endorsement comes after similar frustrations with other Republican senators who opposed him on issues like military intervention in Venezuela and his preferred candidates. The National Republican Senatorial Committee and aligned super PACs have expressed concern that such endorsements could distract from efforts to defeat Democrats in the upcoming elections. Letlow had been hesitant to challenge Cassidy without Trump's public backing, despite meeting with him multiple times over the past year. Big donors had continued to support Cassidy while Letlow remained undecided, even filming commercials for both a House re-election and a potential Senate bid.
