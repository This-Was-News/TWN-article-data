## Article summary
The Pentagon has placed approximately 1,500 active-duty soldiers on standby for a potential deployment to Minnesota. This action is in response to a large-scale immigration enforcement operation being conducted by federal authorities in the state. Two infantry battalions from the Army's 11th Airborne Division, based in Alaska, have received prepare-to-deploy orders. This readiness is contingent on President Donald Trump invoking the Insurrection Act, a seldom-used law that permits the deployment of active-duty troops for law enforcement purposes. This development follows President Trump's recent threats to use the act to suppress protests against his administration's immigration policies. While Trump initially made these threats, he later indicated there was no immediate need for such action. The Insurrection Act has been invoked sporadically throughout history, with the most recent use being in 1992 to address unrest in Los Angeles. Minnesota's Governor, Tim Walz, has publicly appealed to the President to de-escalate the situation and refrain from sending additional troops.
