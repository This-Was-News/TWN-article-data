## Article summary
Parts of Florida experienced a rare snowfall on Sunday, January 18, 2026, for the second consecutive year, as freezing temperatures persisted across the state. The storm system delivered up to 2 inches of snow to southern Alabama and the Florida Panhandle, including Pensacola. This event occurred almost a year after record snowfall in mid-January 2025, when Pensacola received 6 to 8 inches. While the snow has ceased, arctic cold is expected to continue gripping Florida. Orlando and other regions face freeze warnings with temperatures potentially reaching 25 degrees and wind chills in the low 20s. Naples and surrounding areas are under a cold weather advisory with expected wind chills of 29 degrees. Meteorologists describe these cold temperatures and snow as abnormal for Florida but anticipate the cold snap to be short-lived. Temperatures are expected to rise by Tuesday night, with lows returning to the upper 40s.
