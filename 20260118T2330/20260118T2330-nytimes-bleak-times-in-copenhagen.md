## Article summary
Danes are expressing feelings of betrayal, bewilderment, and fear following President Trump's threats to acquire Greenland from Denmark. Henrik Bager, a Danish soldier, described Trump's remarks about Denmark's military as a "punch to the gut." Rasmus Jarlov, chairman of the Danish Parliament's Defense Committee, stated that while Denmark knows the U.S. could destroy them, they would fight back if Trump attacked a NATO ally, though he admitted the notion was "weird."

Casper O. Jensen, a Danish pollster, felt like a "jilted lover," believing Denmark and the U.S. had a strong relationship that Trump has now jeopardized. Greenland, significantly larger than Denmark, is a crucial element of Danish national identity and pride, elevating the small Nordic nation's global standing. Protests against Trump's designs on Greenland have occurred in Denmark and Greenland. The situation has created bleak times in Copenhagen, with citizens feeling unsettled by the American president's aggressive stance.
