## Article summary
The U.S. House of Representatives has passed a package of two government funding bills, known as a minibus, which has now been sent to the Senate for approval. This package allocates a total of $76 billion for various departments and agencies, including the State Department, Treasury, IRS, and national security entities. This marks significant progress in funding the government for fiscal year 2026, with several appropriations bills already signed into law and others passed earlier this month. The national security and foreign affairs bill within the package totals $50 billion, supporting international programs and foreign military financing. The second bill, funding financial services and general government, is authorized at $26.3 billion, with substantial amounts for the Treasury and the Judiciary. Both parties achieved bipartisan wins, with Democrats highlighting increases in election security grants and international humanitarian aid, while Republicans are emphasizing taxpayer savings through spending reductions. The Senate is expected to act on these bills before their upcoming recess to meet a funding deadline. If successful, this would bring the total number of appropriations bills passed to eight, leaving the more contentious ones for later.
