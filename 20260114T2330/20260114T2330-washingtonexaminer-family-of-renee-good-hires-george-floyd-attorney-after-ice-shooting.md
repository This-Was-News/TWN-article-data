## Article summary
The family of Renee Good, who was fatally shot by an Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officer in Minnesota last week, has hired the law firm Romanucci & Blandin. This firm previously represented George Floyd's family in their civil lawsuit against the Minneapolis police officer responsible for his death, which resulted in a $27 million settlement. The announcement was made exactly one week after Good's killing. Attorney Antonio Romanucci stated that they will seek accountability and change in Good's name, emphasizing her desire to be a good person for her family and community. The shooting has sparked protests and led to legal actions, with Minnesota and Illinois suing the federal government over immigration enforcement. The FBI and Minnesota prosecutors are conducting separate investigations into the shooting, and Romanucci & Blandin is launching its own civil investigation. Romanucci highlighted the complexities of filing civil litigation against federal officers under the Federal Tort Claims Act, noting the process involves navigating time-consuming procedures and arguing before a federal judge rather than a jury. Despite these challenges, the firm is committed to pursuing justice for Renee Good's family, which includes her wife, parents, and siblings.
