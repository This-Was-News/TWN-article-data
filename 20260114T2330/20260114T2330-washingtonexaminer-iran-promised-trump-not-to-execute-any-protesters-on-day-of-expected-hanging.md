## Article summary
President Donald Trump announced on Wednesday that Iran has promised not to execute protesters who have been demonstrating against the regime. Trump had previously issued a warning of severe consequences if Iran proceeded with any executions. He stated he was informed on good authority that killings would cease and there were no plans for executions. Trump mentioned that the information reached him just before midnight in Tehran, making any immediate public executions unlikely. The United States will continue to monitor the situation in Iran. One protester, Erfan Sultani, was scheduled for execution on Wednesday after a swift trial, but the hanging was called off. Iran's primary method of execution is hanging, and thousands have been executed in recent years. The protests in Iran, which have been met with brutal force including live ammunition and machine guns, are testing the government's control. Iran has implemented an internet blackout, which could allow for private executions but would also risk exposure if news leaked. The Iranian Justice Minister declared that anyone present on the streets since January 8th is considered a criminal. Trump had previously stated during an interview that strong action would be taken if Iran proceeded with hangings.
