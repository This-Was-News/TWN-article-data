## Article summary
A major winter storm is poised to bring significant snowfall and widespread disruption across the Mid-Atlantic and Northeast regions. Over 160 million Americans are currently under winter weather advisories. Forecasters anticipate heavy snow, strong winds, and hazardous travel conditions that could impact infrastructure and emergency services. Governors in Texas, Louisiana, and Virginia have declared states of emergency in preparation for the severe weather. The National Weather Service warns of dangerous ice from ArkLaTex to the southern Mid-Atlantic, with freezing temperatures potentially prolonging hazardous conditions after the initial snowfall. Meteorologists highlight the potential for this system to be one of the most impactful of the season, with atmospheric conditions aligning for extensive snowfall. Pet owners may face particular challenges due to deep snow accumulation, affecting their animals' ability to go outside. Emergency management officials advise residents to prepare for potential power outages and limited access to essential services. North Carolina's governor urged citizens to stock up on supplies and avoid travel. While the storm's exact track may shift slightly, widespread snowfall and disruption remain expected.
