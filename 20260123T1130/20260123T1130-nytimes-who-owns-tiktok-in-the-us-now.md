## Article summary
TikTok's Chinese parent company, ByteDance, has established a new American venture to manage the popular video app in the United States. This move is in response to a 2024 federal law designed to address national security concerns by separating TikTok from ByteDance due to its ties with Beijing. Non-Chinese investors will collectively own approximately 80 percent of this new American entity, while ByteDance will retain a 19.9 percent share. The new venture will license TikTok's recommendation algorithm from ByteDance and will have the authority to moderate content on the platform. Among the new investors, Oracle, a prominent cloud computing and database software company, holds a 15 percent stake, making it one of the largest shareholders. Larry Ellison, a co-founder of Oracle, serves as its executive chairman and chief technology officer.
