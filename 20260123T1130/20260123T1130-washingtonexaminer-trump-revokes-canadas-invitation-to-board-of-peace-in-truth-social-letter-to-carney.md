## Article summary
President Donald Trump has revoked Canada's invitation to join the Board of Peace, an initiative established at the World Economic Forum summit in Davos. The announcement was made via a letter posted on Truth Social, addressed to Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney. This decision appears to be a response to Carney's earlier speech at the summit, where he emphasized Canada's independence and prosperity. Trump had previously expressed dissatisfaction with Canada's perceived lack of gratitude for U.S. support. Tensions were further heightened by Trump's rhetoric regarding the acquisition of Greenland and his critical stance on NATO. Canada was among several nations invited to join the Board of Peace, which requires a mandatory $1 billion fee for permanent membership. Trump formally unveiled the Board of Peace at a signing ceremony in Davos, with leaders like Hungary's Viktor Orban and Argentina's Javier Milei in attendance. The board's primary objective is to oversee the transition of power in post-war Gaza. Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu accepted the invitation to join but was absent from the signing ceremony, as were any Israeli representatives.
