## Article summary
Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney has publicly responded to U.S. President Donald Trump's assertion that Canada relies on the United States for its existence. Carney stated that Canada thrives due to its own values and identity, emphasizing Canadian sovereignty and self-determination. This exchange occurred after Carney delivered a speech at the World Economic Forum in Davos, where he criticized the coercion of smaller nations by larger powers, though he did not name Trump. Trump retaliated by revoking Carney's invitation to his Board of Peace and suggested Canada should be grateful for U.S. trade benefits. Carney, in turn, highlighted the strong partnership between Canada and the U.S. while asserting Canada's independence. He also spoke about Canada as a model for a pluralistic society that embraces diversity and delivers shared prosperity. California Governor Gavin Newsom expressed support for Carney's stance, viewing it as a courageous act of conviction.
