## Article summary
The U.S. Department of Education has issued guidance encouraging Title I schools to consolidate federal, state, and local funding into a single stream. This initiative aims to reduce red tape and compliance costs, offering greater funding flexibility. Title I funds schools with a high percentage of students from low-income families, with approximately $18 billion spent annually. Schoolwide programs, combined with fund consolidation, empower local decision-makers to better serve students and improve academic achievement by tailoring spending to specific needs like tutoring or technology. This aligns with the Every Student Succeeds Act's goal of shifting federal oversight and promoting school choice and efficiency. The Department of Education stated that consolidation reduces paperwork and accounting burdens for schools. Assistant Secretary Kirsten Baesler highlighted that schoolwide programs break down funding silos, urging leaders to expand eligibility and support for these consolidation efforts. This push for school choice occurs amidst national concerns about educational outcomes. Education departments in California, Nevada, and Texas did not immediately respond to requests for comment on the guidance.
