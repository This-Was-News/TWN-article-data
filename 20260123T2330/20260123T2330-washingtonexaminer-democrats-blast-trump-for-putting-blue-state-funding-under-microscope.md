## Article summary
Democrats are accusing President Donald Trump of political retribution after ordering a review of federal funding to over a dozen Democratic-led states and Washington D.C. This action follows Trump's previous threats to freeze billions in funds over sanctuary policies and alleged fraud. A memo from the Office of Management and Budget (OMB) requests budget data from states like California, New York, and Illinois, among others. Democrats, including Senate Minority Leader Chuck Schumer, view this as intimidation and a tactic to bully political opponents, predicting it will backfire on Republicans. Senator Tim Kaine questioned Trump's focus on "petty political grievances" instead of lowering costs for Americans. The OMB states the review is a data-gathering exercise to identify improper and fraudulent fund usage, not to withhold funds. However, Democrats like Senator Patty Murray and Senator Jeff Merkley have condemned the review as an abuse of power and a "corrupt playbook." The Trump administration has intensified its crackdown on fraud in Democratic states, particularly after a Minnesota scandal. This OMB review is seen by Democrats as an escalation of the White House's attacks on the minority party, with Senator Cory Booker calling it an "agenda of chaos, cruelty, and corruption."
