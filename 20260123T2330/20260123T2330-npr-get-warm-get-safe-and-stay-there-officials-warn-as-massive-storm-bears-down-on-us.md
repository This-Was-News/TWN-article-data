## Article summary
A massive ice and snow storm is projected to impact over 200 million people across the United States, stretching from the Southern Rockies to New England through Monday. The National Weather Service has issued warnings for winter storms, ice, and extreme cold for a significant portion of the country. Governors in states from Texas to New York have declared states of emergency to prepare for widespread travel disruptions, prolonged power outages, and extensive tree damage. Officials are urging residents to prioritize safety, stay indoors, and prepare for potentially days of disruption due to frigid temperatures that will linger into the following week. Freezing rain and ice are of particular concern, as they are expected to accumulate on trees and power lines, leading to electricity loss. The storm has already caused numerous flight cancellations and school closures, with more expected as the system progresses eastward. Many people are stocking up on essential supplies, and local governments are opening inclement weather shelters and warming stations to assist those in need. Authorities emphasize that shelters are open to everyone, regardless of immigration status, to ensure no one is left at risk of freezing.
