## Article summary
The Trump administration has significantly expanded a policy that bans U.S. funding to international groups discussing or providing abortions. This expanded policy, known as the Mexico City policy, now also prohibits aid to organizations that embrace diversity, equity, and inclusion (DEI) or promote "gender ideology." Vice President JD Vance announced the expansion at the March for Life rally, stating the goal is to "protect life" and combat "radical gender ideologies." The policy's scope has broadened from family planning funds to encompass all non-military foreign assistance, potentially affecting over $30 billion. Previously, the policy's reinstatement under Trump led to a $15 million funding cut for MSI Reproductive Choices, impacting reproductive care access for millions. Critics argue this move weaponizes U.S. foreign aid to advance an ideological agenda, making it harder for marginalized groups, including LGBTQ+ individuals, to receive healthcare. The expansion beyond abortion restrictions to include DEI and gender ideology is seen by some as exporting a domestic crusade against these groups abroad. The precise implications are still unclear as specific rules have not yet been released, but advocates fear organizations may self-censor to avoid losing funding, potentially compromising public health and scientific initiatives.
