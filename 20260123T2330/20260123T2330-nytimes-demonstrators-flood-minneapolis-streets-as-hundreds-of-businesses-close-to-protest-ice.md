## Article summary
Hundreds of businesses in Minnesota closed their doors and thousands of protesters shut down parts of Minneapolis and St. Paul in a demonstration against ICE's immigration crackdown. The widespread action, occurring in subzero temperatures, marked the most organized protest since federal agents arrived six weeks prior. Businesses, including locally owned establishments, forfeited a day's revenue to participate in the effort to end the immigration enforcement. This protest followed weeks of clashes between residents and federal agents, which began late last year and resulted in approximately 3,000 arrests. The situation escalated after a U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement agent fatally shot Renee Good, an American citizen, in Minneapolis on January 7. In response, protesters and state officials have filed lawsuits to restrict the agents' conduct and halt the surge of federal agents in the area. The protesters aim to pressure the federal government to withdraw its agents from the streets.
