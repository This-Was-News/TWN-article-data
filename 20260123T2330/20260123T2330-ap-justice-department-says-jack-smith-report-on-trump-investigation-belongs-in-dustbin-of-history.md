## Article summary
The Justice Department has filed a court document stating that the report by former special counsel Jack Smith on his investigation into Donald Trump's handling of classified documents should be "sealed" and remain in the "dustbin of history." Prosecutors argued that the investigation was unlawful and that the report is an "illicit product." This stance aligns with Trump's legal team, who also requested the report be permanently blocked. Smith's two-volume report covered investigations into Trump's efforts to overturn the 2020 election and his retention of classified documents. While the election investigation volume was released, a judge had previously halted the release of the classified documents volume. Smith, in recent testimony, defended his investigations, asserting that he acted without political consideration and that no one is above the law.
