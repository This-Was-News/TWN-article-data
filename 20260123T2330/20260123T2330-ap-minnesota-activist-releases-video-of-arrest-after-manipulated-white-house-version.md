## Article summary
Minnesota activist Nekima Levy Armstrong has released her own video of her arrest, countering a manipulated image posted by the White House on X. The White House image depicted Levy Armstrong crying, with the caption "Arrested far-left agitator Nekima Levy Armstrong for orchestrating church riots in Minnesota." A similar doctored image was shared by Homeland Security secretary Kristi Noem. Levy Armstrong, arrested with at least two others for an anti-Immigration and Customs Enforcement protest at a church, stated in an audio message that the Trump administration used AI to manipulate her arrest photos. Her own video, filmed by her husband, shows her being handcuffed and led into a vehicle, during which she discusses her arrest with agents and asserts it is an abuse of power for speaking out against ICE. The video does not show her crying, contrary to the White House portrayal. Levy Armstrong and another arrested individual, St. Paul school board member Chauntyll Allen, were released on Friday. Levy Armstrong believes they are being politically persecuted for their activism against authoritarianism and fascism.
