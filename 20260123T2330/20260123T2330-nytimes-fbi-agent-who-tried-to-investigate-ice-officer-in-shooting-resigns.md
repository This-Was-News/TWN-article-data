## Article summary
An FBI agent, Tracee Mergen, resigned from her position after facing pressure from bureau leadership to halt a civil rights inquiry into ICE officer Jonathan Ross, who fatally shot Renee Good. Good, an unarmed mother, was killed on January 7th while in her vehicle. Despite initial claims from Trump administration officials that Good attempted to ram Ross, video analysis by The New York Times showed no evidence of this. The Justice Department has stated no plans to investigate whether Ross used excessive force, and federal investigators are refusing to cooperate with state and local prosecutors. Instead of investigating Ross, the Justice Department is scrutinizing Good and her partner for potential ties to left-wing protest groups, a decision that led to the resignation of six senior prosecutors. In related actions, the Justice Department has opened an investigation into Minnesota elected Democrats regarding potential obstruction of immigration enforcement and has begun prosecuting protesters opposing the administration's immigration policies.
