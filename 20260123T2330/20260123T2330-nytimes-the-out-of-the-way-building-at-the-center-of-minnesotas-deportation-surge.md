## Article summary
The B.H. Whipple Federal Building in Minnesota has become a focal point for immigration protests and arrests. For the past six weeks, federal agents have been conducting deportation surges in the Twin Cities, leading to thousands of immigrant arrests. Protests have consistently gathered at the Whipple Building, located between a highway and an airport, rather than in downtown Minneapolis or St. Paul. This 57-year-old building, which houses various government offices including the Department of Homeland Security and an immigration court, is the initial processing center for those apprehended in immigration sweeps. The building's rapid transformation into a rallying point for protesters and a detention center for immigrants has been significant. Detainees have reported crowded conditions, families huddled together, and widespread distress.
