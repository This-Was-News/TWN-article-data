## Article summary
NASA's Crew-11 mission concluded with a splashdown off San Diego after five months aboard the International Space Station. The mission was shortened by about a month due to a medical issue experienced by one of the four crew members. This marks the first time in NASA's history that a medical condition has prompted an early return from a space mission and the first medical evacuation from the ISS in over 25 years. The astronaut's name and specific medical issue have not been disclosed due to privacy concerns. The entire crew, including NASA astronauts Zena Cardman and Mike Fincke, JAXA astronaut Kimiya Yui, and Roscosmos cosmonaut Oleg Platonov, had to return as their SpaceX Dragon capsule was their only means of transport. The mission began on August 1, 2025, and the crew spent 165 days on the space station, completing 2,672 orbits. Their early departure reduces the ISS crew to three, potentially impacting ongoing experiments and maintenance. The next crew rotation, SpaceX's Crew-12, is scheduled to launch in mid-February.
