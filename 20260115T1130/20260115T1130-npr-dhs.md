## Article summary
Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) officers in Minneapolis shot a Venezuelan man in the leg on January 15, 2026, after he allegedly resisted arrest and assaulted an officer. According to the Department of Homeland Security (DHS), two other individuals then attacked the officer with a snow shovel and broom handle. The shooting occurred shortly before Minnesota Governor Tim Walz addressed the state about an increase in immigration agents and urged residents to record their actions. This incident follows another ICE-involved shooting of a U.S. citizen in Minneapolis just a week prior, which had already heightened tensions. Protesters gathered at the scene of the latest shooting, leading to clashes with federal agents who used irritants and flashbangs to disperse the crowd. Residents expressed fear for their safety and concerns about the impact of federal agents on their community, with one stating that their favorite restaurants had closed due to employees' fear of ICE encounters. The presence of federal agents has reportedly created a "war zone" atmosphere in the city, intended to intimidate residents.
