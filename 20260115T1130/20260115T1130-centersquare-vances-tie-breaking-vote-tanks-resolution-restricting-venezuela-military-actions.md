## Article summary
The U.S. Senate narrowly defeated a resolution aimed at limiting military actions in Venezuela, with Vice President J.D. Vance casting the tie-breaking vote. Initially, five Republicans supported advancing the war powers resolution, but two, Sens. Josh Hawley and Todd Young, changed their votes. Sen. Rand Paul criticized the ongoing military blockade of Venezuela, arguing it constitutes war. Secretary of State Marco Rubio has committed to updating the Senate Foreign Relations Committee on the situation. The resolution was introduced by Democrats following a large-scale strike by the Trump administration against the Venezuelan government, which resulted in the arrest of Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro and his wife. Subsequently, the U.S. seized two sanctioned Venezuelan vessels. Maduro and his wife are facing charges in New York, including narco-terrorism conspiracy and drug importation, and have pleaded not guilty.
