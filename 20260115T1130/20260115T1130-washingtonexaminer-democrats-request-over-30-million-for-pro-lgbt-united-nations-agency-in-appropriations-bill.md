## Article summary
Democrats on the Senate Appropriations Committee are requesting over $30 million for the United Nations Population Fund (UNFPA) as part of a broader appropriations bill. While the UNFPA engages in generally uncontroversial activities like maternal healthcare and reducing gender-based violence, it also supports pro-LGBTQIA+ initiatives. The agency partners with transgender activists and LGBT activists in various countries for sexual education and community engagement, and sponsors safe spaces for diverse sexual orientations and gender identities. The UNFPA's website states a commitment to securing dignity and rights for LGBTQIA+ people and achieving lasting equality. This pro-LGBTQIA+ focus could lead to opposition from Republicans, who have previously criticized such operations. However, the proposed legislation includes provisions for compromise, such as requiring U.S. funds to be held in a separate account, allowing for conditions to be attached. One condition already present is a dollar-for-dollar reduction in U.S. funding for any amount the UNFPA spends in China. Additionally, the legislation stipulates that UNFPA funding will be withheld if the agency funds abortions. Key members of the Senate Appropriations Committee did not respond to requests for comment on the matter.
