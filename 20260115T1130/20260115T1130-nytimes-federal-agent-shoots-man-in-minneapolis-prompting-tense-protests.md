## Article summary
A federal agent shot a man from Venezuela in Minneapolis on Wednesday evening while attempting to detain him, sparking hours of clashes between protesters and law enforcement. According to a Department of Homeland Security spokeswoman, the man was in the country illegally, fled from agents, and then violently assaulted an officer along with two other individuals using a snow shovel and a broom handle. The agent, fearing for his life, shot the man in the leg. The incident occurred just one week after an immigration agent fatally shot an American woman in the city, leading to ongoing protests. Protesters gathered near the scene, confronting police and demanding the arrest of federal agents. Law enforcement deployed gas and chemical agents against the crowd, while protesters threw fireworks and snowballs at federal agents. The mayor expressed concern about the escalating situation and reiterated his call for ICE to leave the city. The Minnesota Bureau of Criminal Apprehension has been asked to investigate the shooting. The deployment of federal immigration agents in Minneapolis has angered residents and local officials, who view their presence as harmful rather than beneficial for public safety.
