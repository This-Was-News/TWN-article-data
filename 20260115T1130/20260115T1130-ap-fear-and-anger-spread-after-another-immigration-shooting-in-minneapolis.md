## Article summary
A federal officer shot a man in the leg in Minneapolis after reportedly being attacked with a shovel and broom handle, escalating tensions following a week of protests against immigration enforcement. This incident occurred just a week after an immigration agent fatally shot a woman, Renee Good, during a large-scale immigration crackdown. Protesters clashed with federal officers, who deployed tear gas, while the Minneapolis Police Chief declared the gathering an unlawful assembly. The city's mayor described the situation as unsustainable, with a large federal force present and local police still responsible for daily safety. The Department of Homeland Security stated the man shot was in the U.S. illegally, fled from officers, and was then attacked by the man and two others. Meanwhile, a lawsuit filed by Minnesota and its cities seeks to suspend the immigration crackdown, with a judge granting the government time to respond. The governor characterized the federal actions as a campaign of organized brutality. The Pentagon is reportedly seeking military lawyers to assist in the federal immigration operations. The ICE officer who shot Renee Good reportedly suffered internal bleeding, and her family has hired the same law firm that represented George Floyd's family.
