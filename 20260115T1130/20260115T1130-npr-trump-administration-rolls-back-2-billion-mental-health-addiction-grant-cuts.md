## Article summary
The Trump administration is reversing its decision to cut over $2 billion in grants for mental health and addiction programs. These cuts, which were announced via letters sent late Tuesday night, had surprised and alarmed care providers across the United States. The decision to terminate the funding had been met with significant political backlash from both Republicans and Democrats. Following this negative response, high-level meetings within the administration led to the reversal of the decision. Grant money will now be restored to more than 2,000 organizations and grant recipients. Public health advocates expressed relief but remained shaken by the chaotic events. They highlighted the bipartisan support in Congress for mental health initiatives. The termination letters stated that the programs did not align with the administration's public health agenda. The exact individuals responsible for the initial cuts and the subsequent reversal remain undetermined.
