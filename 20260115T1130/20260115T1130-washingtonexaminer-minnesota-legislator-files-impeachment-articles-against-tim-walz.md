## Article summary
Minnesota State Rep. Mike Wiener has filed articles of impeachment against Governor Tim Walz, citing alleged corrupt conduct and violations of his oath of office. These actions are a response to revelations of significant fraud within Minnesota's state-administered programs, with an estimated $9 billion lost to taxpayers. Wiener claims Walz knowingly concealed or allowed the concealment of this widespread fraud despite repeated warnings and audits. The impeachment articles also accuse Walz of failing to take timely and effective action, delaying corrective reforms, and not holding responsible officials accountable. Additionally, Walz is accused of prioritizing political narratives over disclosing program failures and allowing agencies to operate without adequate safeguards after fraud risks were identified. Ten Republican colleagues in the House are supporting Wiener's efforts. The Minnesota state legislature reconvenes in February, and for the impeachment articles to advance, they require a majority vote in the House and a two-thirds majority in the Senate to remove Walz from office. Notably, Governor Walz recently withdrew his bid for reelection following the fraud revelations.
