## Article summary
Senior Trump administration officials, including Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem and Corey Lewandowski, have allegedly waged an aggressive campaign to force U.S. Customs and Border Protection Commissioner Rodney Scott out of his position. Sources claim these efforts stem from disagreements over deportation goals and ethical concerns, with tactics described as "evil" and negatively impacting staff families. Noem and Lewandowski reportedly view Scott, a 30-year veteran, as a threat due to his challenges to their enforcement approaches. The campaign allegedly involves promoting loyalists and pressuring Scott's inner circle, including the abrupt firing of HR head Andrea Bright and the reassignment of Scott's chief of staff and deputy. These actions occur amid White House pressure to increase deportations and build the border wall. Despite these internal conflicts, official statements from the White House and DHS assert a united front, with all team members working seamlessly to implement the President's agenda. Scott, in a statement, also emphasized the administration's success in securing the border under his leadership.
