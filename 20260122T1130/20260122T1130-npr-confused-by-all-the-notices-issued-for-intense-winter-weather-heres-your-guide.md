## Article summary
Much of the United States is preparing for a significant winter storm and extreme cold this weekend. The National Weather Service (NWS) has issued various notices to inform the public, with definitions and criteria sometimes varying by region due to differing climate norms. A Winter Storm Watch indicates a medium confidence that a storm is approaching, prompting early preparation for potential heavy snow, sleet, or ice. A Winter Storm Warning is issued when there is high confidence of severe winter weather, urging immediate completion of preparations. Extreme Cold Watches are issued when dangerously cold temperatures or wind chills are possible but not precisely located, advising precautions like avoiding outdoor exposure and maintaining vehicle fuel levels. Extreme Cold Warnings are issued when these dangerous conditions are expected or occurring, recommending staying indoors and dressing in layers if venturing out. Winter Weather Advisories are for lighter wintry precipitation or blowing snow, cautioning drivers to be mindful of road conditions. Blizzard Warnings are issued for imminent or occurring high-risk conditions, including heavy snow, strong winds, and severely reduced visibility, strongly advising against travel and recommending staying with a vehicle if stranded.
