## Article summary
The American Legislative Exchange Council (ALEC) has released its annual Index of State Education Freedom, ranking states based on their support for school choice programs. Florida secured the top position overall, with Arizona and Arkansas following closely behind. The index assesses states across five categories: student-centered funding, charter schools, home schools, virtual schools, and open enrollment. Florida's Commissioner of Education, Anastasios Kamoutsas, stated that the state leads because it provides all students and families with access to high-quality school choice options tailored to their individual needs. Conversely, Kentucky and New York were ranked at the bottom, with Nebraska listed last. Nebraska, despite a high per-pupil cost, has a significant number of students enrolled in public schools. The ALEC Index aims to inform parents and policymakers about the landscape of education freedom in each state and suggest improvements to expand learning opportunities. This initiative aligns with the broader trend of states moving towards education freedom, where students can choose the learning model that best suits them, empowering parents to make these decisions rather than the government. National School Choice Week, from January 25-31, is dedicated to raising awareness about these choices through various events and advocacy.
