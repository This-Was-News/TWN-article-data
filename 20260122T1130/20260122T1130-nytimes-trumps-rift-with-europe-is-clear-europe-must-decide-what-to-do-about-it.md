## Article summary
President Trump's disdain for Europe's immigration policies, regulations, and its refusal to sell Greenland to the U.S. was evident in his remarks at Davos. Despite hopes for a diplomatic resolution, with Trump announcing a potential deal with NATO's secretary general regarding Greenland and the deferral of threatened tariffs, no details have emerged. A Danish parliament member from Greenland expressed confusion over the proposed deal, questioning its validity. This situation highlights a significant rift between the U.S. and Europe, with fundamental differences in values between European governments and the Trump administration. The trans-Atlantic relationship is described as being in a "very rocky place," indicating a lack of common ground between once-close allies.
