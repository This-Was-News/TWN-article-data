## Article summary
An autopsy has ruled the death of Cuban immigrant Geraldo Lunas Campos, who was in ICE custody, as a homicide due to asphyxia. Campos died on January 3rd at an ICE detention facility in El Paso, Texas, after an altercation with guards. While ICE initially claimed he was attempting suicide and staff tried to save him, a witness stated that guards held him down, and one applied pressure to his neck until he became unconscious. The autopsy report detailed signs of a struggle and injuries consistent with physical restraint, including hemorrhages on his neck and petechial hemorrhages in his eyes. This death is one of at least three reported at the Camp East Montana facility in a little over a month. ICE's account of the events has changed, and a spokesperson later emphasized Campos's criminal history, including a child sex predator conviction. The circumstances surrounding these deaths have drawn scrutiny to the Camp East Montana facility, which is operated by a private contractor with no prior experience in corrections. A congresswoman has called for the facility to be shut down and for an investigation into the deaths, urging that all evidence be preserved, including the deportation of witnesses. Another detainee from Guatemala died at the facility from suspected liver and kidney failure, and a third from Nicaragua died of a presumed suicide.
