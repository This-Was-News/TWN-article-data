## Article summary
The Arizona Senate Public Safety Committee has voted 4-3 along party lines to support a bill that would rename highway Loop 202 as the Charlie Kirk Loop 202. This legislation, Senate Bill 1010, now moves to the full Senate for consideration. The 77-mile loop encircles the Phoenix area and connects to Interstate 10. The bill sponsor, Senate President Warren Petersen, R-Gilbert, stated that segments of the loop would retain their previous designations, addressing concerns that a portion honoring the late U.S. Rep. Ed Pastor would be removed. Petersen emphasized that the segment honoring Pastor would still be named after him. The renaming aims to commemorate Kirk's contributions to civic engagement and free speech. Kirk, an Arizonan, was assassinated in September while speaking at a rally in Utah. The man accused of his murder, Tyler James Robinson, faces multiple felony charges, and the prosecution is seeking the death penalty. Kirk's family and supporters believe his death underscores the importance of civil discourse. The committee's Republican members voted in favor of SB 1010, while the Democratic members opposed it.
