## Article summary
Some voters who supported Donald Trump in the 2024 election are expressing concerns that Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) is acting too aggressively, particularly after a recent fatal shooting incident in Minneapolis. In online focus groups with 14 swing voters from Pennsylvania, who had previously voted for Biden in 2020 before switching to Trump, opinions were divided on ICE's actions. While eight voters felt ICE was performing appropriately, six believed the agency had "gone too far." There was a notable awareness and discussion surrounding the shooting of Renee Macklin Good, with differing views on who bore the most blame for the incident. Some voters expressed frustration with the perceived lack of focus on economic issues by the Trump administration, preferring a stronger emphasis on domestic concerns like the economy over international affairs. Many participants voiced opposition to the use of force in acquiring territories like Greenland. Overall, a majority of the focus group participants disapproved of Trump's job performance, though criticism was also directed at Democrats, who were described as "weak" and "broken."
