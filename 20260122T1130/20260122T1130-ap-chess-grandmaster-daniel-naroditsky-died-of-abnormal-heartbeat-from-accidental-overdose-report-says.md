## Article summary
American chess grandmaster Daniel Naroditsky, aged 29, died from an abnormal heartbeat caused by an accidental overdose, according to a toxicology report. The report revealed the presence of methamphetamine and kratom in his system at the time of his death in October. Naroditsky was last seen alive on October 18th and found deceased the following day after missing a flight. Friends had previously removed 40 Adderall pills from his home after he exhibited strange online behavior, and bags of kratom were also discovered. Naroditsky, a child prodigy who became a grandmaster at 18, was known for popularizing chess through livestreams on YouTube and Twitch. He had recently faced accusations of cheating from former World Chess Champion Vladimir Kramnik, which he denied. Naroditsky stated in his last livestream that these allegations had taken a toll on him. The International Chess Federation filed a complaint against Kramnik for harassment, while Kramnik denied any factual link to Naroditsky's death and reported receiving death threats.
