## Article summary
Space Forces Southern was officially activated on Wednesday at Davis-Monthan Air Force Base near Tucson, Arizona. This new unit will serve as the space component of U.S. Southern Command, integrating space capabilities across the Western Hemisphere. It will be the main base for collaborative space operations in the Caribbean, Central America, and South America, aiming to merge space power with partners to support regional security, deterrence, and stability. Colonel Brandon P. Alford was named its first commander, expressing optimism about the growing operational importance of space. He stated that the command will shape the space domain to strengthen shared values and aspirations. Chief of Space Operations General Chance Saltzman highlighted the activation as a reaffirmation of the U.S. commitment to addressing threats from various actors. He emphasized that Space Forces Southern will be a force for good, using space to maintain peace, stability, and defend the homeland. The official emblem, featuring the Southern Cross constellation and a lightning bolt, was also debuted, symbolizing speed and responsiveness in operations focused on Central and South America. The activation underscores the idea of a stronger, unified hemisphere connected by shared challenges and opportunities.
